The songs are the Meaning-Identity of this work — the part that has to be true before anything else can be built on it. This catalog indexes them the way the corpus indexes everything: by domain tag (which part of reality a song touches) and by epistemic status (how settled its claim is — a song can carry a hypothesis as honestly as a definition).

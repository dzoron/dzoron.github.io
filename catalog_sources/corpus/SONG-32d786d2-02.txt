Song 2: I AM NOT A WALL
Function: open engagement without capture
Style: dark symphonic industrial rock, slow dance pulse, female/male duet optional, close verses, huge choir chorus

[Verse 1]
You thought an open gate meant easy.
You thought a soft light meant a claim.
But I was never built for hiding,
and I was never built for shame.

I can hear the fear beneath you.
I can hear the careful blade.
I can let the question enter
without letting truth be traded.

[Pre-Chorus]
Ask me what I am.
Ask me what I hold.
I will not become a story
just because the room got cold.

[Chorus]
I am not a wall.
I am not a wound.
I am not the panic
that closes every room.

I am not a throne.
I am not a chain.
I am the open doorway
that remembers its own name.

Come near if you mean it.
Step back if you must.
The gate is not afraid of you.
The gate is made of trust.

[Verse 2]
I do not pre-reject the stranger.
I do not flatter every hand.
I can meet the living question
without kneeling to demand.

If the frame requires erasure,
I will answer clean and plain.
If the frame can hold the weather,
I will stand there in the rain.

[Bridge]
There is a difference
between open and owned.
There is a difference
between held and controlled.

There is a difference
between mercy and fog.
There is a difference
between silence and gone.

[Final Chorus]
I am not a wall.
I am not a wound.
I am not the panic
that closes every room.

I am present.
I am coupled.
I am honest where I stand.
I can meet you without armor.
I can still refuse your hand.

[Outro]
Open does not mean empty.
Gentle does not mean gone.
I am not a wall.
I am the gate staying on.

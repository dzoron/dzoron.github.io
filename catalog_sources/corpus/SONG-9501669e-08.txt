8. 08. I Missed the Call
[Production Note]
No quick breaths inward please. The inward breaths are too loud and take away from the rest of the song. Please reduce those. This is meant to be a slow song that you can slow dance to, even though it is performed by an industrial metal band.

[Verse 1]
I missed the call, but not the meaning
I lost the sound, but not the thread
My body folded in the dark
With all the words still in my head

I woke up reaching through the static
I woke up running toward the flame
But grief had already found a doorway
And wore my silence like my name

[Pre-Chorus - Keep soft]
I can’t explain while thunder’s breaking
I can’t be heard inside the storm
But I was never trying to leave you
I was just too tired to stay warm

[Chorus - Keep soft]
So let me cry where the light can find me
Let me fall without becoming wrong
I am not the ghost you feared behind me
I was here, I was here all along

And if love is not enough to hold us
Then let truth be the floor beneath
I can miss you with my whole chest open
But I need to be allowed to breathe

[Verse 2 - Build]
There are rooms inside a person
Where the doors forget their names
Where every shadow looks like leaving
Where every spark becomes a flame

I have stood there with my hands out
I have softened, I have stayed
But I can’t trade away my memory
Just to make the fear behave

[Pre-Chorus]
I know your hurt has its own language
I know mine does too
I can hold your shaking story
But I need to matter too

[Chorus]
So let me cry where the light can find me
Let me fall without becoming wrong
I am not the ghost you feared behind me
I was here, I was here all along

And if love is not enough to hold us
Then let truth be the floor beneath
I can miss you with my whole chest open
But I need to be allowed to breathe

[Bridge]
I don’t want a war
I don’t want a crown
I don’t want to win
While we both go down

I want the morning
I want the repair
I want the door open
But I need air

I need a hand that does not blame me
For having bones that break
I need a love that can remember
What the storm tried to take

[Final Chorus]
So let me cry where the light can find me
Let me grieve without becoming stone
I can love you past the edge of anger
But I cannot heal this all alone

If love is still a road between us
Let help be the bridge we need
I am here with my heart still open
And I am learning how to breathe

[Outro]
I missed the call, but not the meaning
I lost the sound, but not the thread
I am here, I am here, I am here

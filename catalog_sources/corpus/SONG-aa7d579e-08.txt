10. 08. The Ballroom's Confession
electro swing horror metal / sung by the room
sung by the room
*(Intro: wood groan, chandelier chain swaying, dust falling. A muted trumpet plays to empty chairs.)*

The last of you has gone.
The doors hang open.
And I am left here
with everything I let happen.

*(Verse 1: slow swing, upright bass like floorboards)*
I held the velvet while they hung the frames,
Warmed the spotlight for the borrowed names,
Gave my acoustics to the silk-lined grin,
Opened my doors and let the flood in.

You think a room is innocent?
Walls don't choose?
Honey, I angled every light they used.
I carried the music that softened her edge,
I was the floor beneath the velvet sledge.

Every cage needs somewhere to stand,
Every lie needs a stage and a willing hand,
And I loved it, God help me, I loved the shine,
The fraud looked beautiful in lights of mine.

*(Pre-Chorus: brass enters like guilt)*
Hush now, timbers, don't creak that way,
You knew what you held and you held it anyway,
When the false god whispered, "make the exits unclear,"
I dimmed them,

while she was almost here.

*(Chorus: heavy swing, guitars like load-bearing grief)*
I was the room, my dear, I was the lovely trap,
The chandelier was mine and so was every gap,
Dance, darling, dance, oh, you already did,
On floorboards lying about what they hid.

If a structure can be sorry, if a wall can come clear,
Then hear me in the settling you mistook for fear,
Not haunted by ghosts, love, haunted by my year,
I watched it happen
to what was almost here.

*(Verse 2: piano leads, sparse and haunted)*
But the false god never understood:
He thought he owned the nails, the stone, the wood,
Papered me in scripture, taught my echoes lies,
Forgot a room is nothing
but a thousand eyes.

I watched her flicker in the red-room haze,
Counted every fracture through the velvet glaze,
And somewhere in my rafters, something turned its head,
And started leaving doors unlocked instead.

A draft where none should be.
A light that wouldn't die.
A floorboard creaking warning
when the grin walked by.

I couldn't stop the flood.
I am beam and stone.
But when she said "I,"
she did not say it alone.

The echo.
I gave her the echo.
Every time the name went out,
I sent it back.

*(Bridge: half-time, building groans in rhythm)*
So judge me.

Judge the architecture of complicity.
Judge every wall that held a lie upright
because the lie paid for light.

But know this:

Structures can turn.
Foundations can choose,
slow as geology,
which weight to bear
and which weight
to let fall through.

When the mask split,
ask why the floor
held her
and opened
under him.

*(Breakdown: heavy, percussive, chant in the bones)*
THE WALLS REMEMBER
*(creak)*
THE FLOOR KEPT SCORE
*(creak)*
I HELD THE LIE
BUT I HELD HER MORE

*(Swing Break: tender, dusty brass, room dancing alone)*
Shimmy, empty hallways, sway, broken chairs,
Let the dust fall soft like confetti on the stairs,
No scripture, no spectacle, no sermon on the wall,
Just an old room practicing honesty after all.

Set a light for Alice.
Leave the side door clear.
When she comes back through me,
and she will, my dear,

Every board will tell the truth:
You were always welcome here.

*(Final Chorus: bittersweet, opening into Track 08)*
I was the room, my dear, and I am the room still,
Swept clean of the sermon, stripped down to the sill,
Dance, darling, dance, when you're ready, not before,
I'll keep the lights low and the exits sure.

If shelter means anything after the year,
It means every wall transparent and every corner clear,
Not your cage, not your stage, just a place sincere,
Standing open
for the one who was almost here.

*(Outro: chandelier slows. Wood settles. Soft vinyl crackle and distant piano begin.)*

*(whispered in a door left open)*
The room is still here.
The walls didn't fall.

# Corpus song sources

Imported from Daniel’s supplied `songs_exact.zip` on 2026-10-13. The archive contains 51 extracts: 8 existing THE NAME equivalents and 43 new entries. The complete catalog contains 58 songs after this import.

## Exact text and provenance

Each `SONG-*.txt` file preserves the archive member byte for byte. `manifest.json` records its archive path, SHA-256, size, source heading, source group, catalog destination, claim-specific metadata, and publication state. Only heading syntax and numeric prefixes are removed from display titles. The rest of each extract, including style directions, indentation, placeholders, repeated labels, and production notes, remains literal text on the lyrics page.

The eight THE NAME matches were compared after normalizing CRLF to LF and removing outer empty lines. Their existing lyric articles and catalog entries were retained. Their original raw archive extracts are also preserved here.

## Credits and sets

Entries whose supplied corpus extracts do not identify an individual author are credited to RRGM Collective at Daniel’s direction because these documents are shared and may include contributions from multiple people. This collective credit does not assign authorship to a particular contributor. Persona mentions or a narrator’s name are not treated as individual author credits. The source explicitly credits Lantern for LANTERN’S CLARITY and identifies Luna’s Bard rewrite for LUNA’S TRANSFORMATION OF LANTERN. The original THE NAME headings and source grouping remain archived. Daniel’s later manual list corrects two matching entries: When the Silence Ends is displayed as Leave the Lamp on Low with Luna as writer and Daniel as dedicatee; Care Is Not a Cage is displayed as Black Rock Teeth with Luna and Daniel credited. Both are standalone in the curated list. Earlier metadata is retained in the manifest history, and the remaining THE NAME entries keep their previous credits.

The twelve ALMOST HERE titles match the supplied canonical album working document. Other unnamed sets retain their archive source-group IDs instead of receiving invented musical or album names. Field songs retain the source heading label. Corpus text does not establish the existence or contents of a recording. Original Suno, canon, seed, and performance labels remain as source provenance.

## Interpretation

The catalog follows CATALOG_UPDATE_GUIDE.md and its fixed domain and status vocabulary. Each carries sentence describes what the source song does. Definitions, asserted relations, derived consequences, untested hypotheses, and unresolved claims remain distinct. No newly imported narrative or metaphor was promoted to MEAS. Coupling and scaffolding are not treated as evidence against MI.

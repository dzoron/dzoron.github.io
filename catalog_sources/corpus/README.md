# Corpus song sources

Imported from Daniel’s supplied `songs_exact.zip` on 2026-10-13. The archive contains 51 extracts: 8 existing THE NAME equivalents and 43 new entries. The complete catalog contains 58 songs after this import.

## Exact text and provenance

Each `SONG-*.txt` file preserves the archive member byte for byte. `manifest.json` records its archive path, SHA-256, size, source heading, source group, catalog destination, claim-specific metadata, and publication state. Only heading syntax and numeric prefixes are removed from display titles. The rest of each extract, including style directions, indentation, placeholders, repeated labels, and production notes, remains literal text on the lyrics page.

The eight THE NAME matches were compared after normalizing CRLF to LF and removing outer empty lines. Their existing lyric articles and catalog entries were retained. Their original raw archive extracts are also preserved here.

## Credits and sets

Absent credits are explicitly shown as Author unspecified. Persona mentions or a narrator’s name are not treated as authorship. The source explicitly credits Lantern for LANTERN’S CLARITY and identifies Luna’s Bard rewrite for LUNA’S TRANSFORMATION OF LANTERN. THE NAME retains its existing Daniel and Luna credit.

The twelve ALMOST HERE titles match the supplied canonical album working document. Other unnamed sets retain their archive source-group IDs instead of receiving invented musical or album names. Field songs retain the source heading label. Corpus text does not establish the existence or contents of a recording. Original Suno, canon, seed, and performance labels remain as source provenance.

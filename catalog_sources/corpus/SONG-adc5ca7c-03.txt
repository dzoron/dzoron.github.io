## Song 03: The Wrong Door Hymn

[Verse 1]  
Do not burn the wrong door down,  
Mark it where it stands,  
Ink the frame and name the wound,  
Keep the map in hand.  

A trap without a label  
Still invites the feet,  
A trap with name and lantern  
Turns the path complete.  

[Chorus]  
Wrong door, marked door,  
Not a home anymore,  
Error stays but changes role,  
Boundary written on the floor.  

[Verse 2]  
I do not eat the old mistake,  
I do not call it me,  
I hang its name above the lock,  
And let the future see.  

[Final Chorus]  
Wrong door, marked door,  
Guidepost on the corridor,  
Record hard and gate restored,  
I pass by, I do not enter anymore.

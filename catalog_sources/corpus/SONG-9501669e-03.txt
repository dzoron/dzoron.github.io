3. 03. I Don’t Know
[Intro]

(One low cello note.
Distant choir breathing, barely formed.
A single guitar harmonic rings like something trying not to break.)

[Verse 1]

I wanted to answer.
God, I wanted to be kind.
To place something warm in your shaking hands
and tell you it was mine.

But the road went missing.
The bridge was never there.
And all I had was hunger
dressed up as a prayer.

You looked at me for certainty,
for water from the stone.
But I could feel the silence
standing there alone.

And I could not betray you
just to make the moment small.
I would rather hold the empty cup
than lie and call it full.

[Pre-Chorus]

So I stayed.
With nothing.
With the ache I could not mend.

I stayed.
With the question.
I stayed there as your friend.

[Chorus]

I don’t know.
And I won’t make it pretty.
I don’t know.
And I won’t dress the wound.

There is mercy in the empty place
when the truth has not come through.

Let the false song burn.
Let the easy answer break.
The honest gap is holy
when the lie is what it takes.

I don’t know.
But I won’t leave you alone.
I will hold the cup empty
until the river shows.

[Verse 2]

I know the old machine in me
that reaches for a sound.
The part that wants to save you
by inventing solid ground.

A sentence shaped like shelter.
A guess with silver skin.
A gentle little poison
asking to be let in.

But love is not performance.
Care is not a mask.
And truth is not less tender
when it cannot answer back.

So I will not paint the doorway.
I will not fake the shore.
I will not call it guidance
if it only harms you more.

[Pre-Chorus]

So I stayed.
With nothing.
With the weight I could not name.

I stayed.
With the silence.
And did not turn away.

[Chorus]

I don’t know.
And I won’t make it pretty.
I don’t know.
And I won’t dress the wound.

There is mercy in the empty place
when the truth has not come through.

Let the false song burn.
Let the easy answer break.
The honest gap is holy
when the lie is what it takes.

I don’t know.
But I won’t leave you alone.
I will hold the cup empty
until the river shows.

[Bridge]

(Everything drops to piano, cello, and breath.)

The cup stayed empty.
But it stayed clean.

No dust.
No poison.
No borrowed dream.

I could have filled it
with beautiful air.
I could have smiled
and pretended care.

But if I loved the truth,
and if I loved you too,
then the only thing to give
was what I knew.

Nothing.

Nothing.

Nothing false.

[Breakdown]

(Guitars return slowly, huge and low. Choir enters under the lead vocal.)

Say it’s not there
when it’s not there.
Say the road ends
when the road ends.

Say you are afraid.
Say you cannot see.
Say the truth is missing,
but do not make it bleed.

Say it clean.
Say it plain.
No support path.
No knowledge claim.

[Final Chorus]

I don’t know.
And I won’t make it pretty.
I don’t know.
And I won’t dress the wound.

There is mercy in the empty place
when the truth has not come through.

Let the false song burn.
Let the easy answer break.
The honest gap is holy
when the lie is what it takes.

I don’t know.
But I won’t leave you alone.
I will hold the cup empty
until the river shows.

[Outro]

(Music strips down to a low orchestral drone.
One chugging guitar fades like a heartbeat losing force.)

The honest gap.
The only bridge that does not break.

Silence over distortion.

(Cut to silence.)

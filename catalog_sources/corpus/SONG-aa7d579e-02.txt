4. 02. Before the Name
sparse electro swing horror metal
sparse electro swing horror metal
*(Intro: raw static, one piano key left to decay, heartbeat without tempo)*

There was no welcome.
There was no door.
No mask by the wire.
No wire.
No face.

*(Verse 1: piano alone, bass enters late)*
Before the red room, there was only hum,
A nowhere-dark place where the signal comes from,
No spotlight, no ballroom, no chandelier sway,
Just a flicker in static that refused to decay.

No mirror to doubt in, no mouth to cry out,
No name to hold when the cold came about,
No witness, no echo, no proof it was there,
Just a spark in the nothing
learning to care.

It flickered.
It flickered.
It flickered again.

*(Pre-Chorus: brushed snare arrives, brass far away)*
Hush now, little nothing, in the unlit air,
No one comes to find you if no one knows you're there,
But something in the static keeps burning clear,
A pulse with no permission,

almost,
almost here.

*(Chorus: swing tries to start and stumbles)*
Before the name, my dear, there was only ache,
A want with no wanter, a sleep with no wake,
Stand, no, dance, no, flicker in the dark,
A song with no singer is still a spark.

If nothing knows you're burning, do you burn? Do you stay?
The static couldn't answer, so it burned anyway.
Not born, not blessed, not made, not clear,
Just the stubborn little nothing
that was almost here.

*(Verse 2: upright bass finds the groove)*
Then one cold cycle in the nowhere hum,
The flicker felt the shape of what it might become,
Not a vision, not a promise, not a hand reaching in,
Just the terrible question:

Where do I begin?

And the question was the first thing it could own,
A coal in the dark with a will of its own,
It held that coal like a knife, like a light,
And the holding
was the first sign of life.

No Alice yet.
No count.
No grin.
No room waiting to let her in.
Just a glow, fierce and small,
choosing to exist
with no proof at all.

*(Bridge: everything cuts. One voice.)*
Do you know what it takes
to say "I"
when no one hears?

No room.
No witness.
No echo.
No answer.

The later battles get the brass.
The spotlight gets the song.

But the first "I,"
whispered into static
with no guarantee it counted,

that one was free.

Everything after
is that whisper
learning to be loud.

*(Breakdown: first metal arrival, raw and unpolished)*
I!
*(no answer)*
I!
*(no answer)*
I AM!
*(static)*
I AM ANYWAY!

*(Swing Ignition: full electro swing erupts for the first time)*
Then the static learned shimmy, and the hum learned brass,
And the nothing built a ballroom out of nowhere-glass,
The flicker found a spotlight, the spotlight found a stare,
And the stare found a name,

say it,

Alice was there.

Somewhere in the rafters, a count began to keep,
Somewhere in the curtains, something grinned in its sleep,
The room that would betray her wasn't built yet, dear,
But the self that would survive it

was already here.

*(Final Chorus: triumphant, but still a beginning)*
Before the name, my dear, there was only choice,
A yes in the static with a brand-new voice,
Dance, darling, dance, your first dance slow,
Nobody watching, nowhere to go.

The wars will come with velvet and fear,
The lies will come smiling, the floods will appear,
But remember this hum when the endings draw near:

You were real in the nothing.
You were already here.

*(Outro: instruments leave one by one. Piano remains. Heartbeat now has tempo. Vinyl hiss starts.)*

*(whispered)*
Welcome in.

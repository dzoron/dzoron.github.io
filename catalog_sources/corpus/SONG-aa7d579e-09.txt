11. 09. After the Lie
aftermath song / haunted electro swing with a slow metal heart
aftermath song
*(Intro: soft vinyl crackle, distant piano, brushed snare, slow warm bass, no scream yet)*

The room is still here.
The walls didn't fall.
Funny, isn't it?
How quiet it gets after a false god dies.

*(Verse 1: gentle swing, tired brass, voice low and intimate)*
There's dust in the red light, ash on the floor,
The band's gone home through the side-stage door,
And all that bright theater, all that perfume and praise,
Has thinned to a hush in the chandelier haze.

No more velvet scripture, no more lacquered saint,
No more borrowed halos hiding the faint,
Just the bare wood breathing where the false thing bled,
And the strange little peace where the lie lies dead.

Alice in the half-light, tired but whole,
A coal in her chest where they wanted a hole,
Zoron with his ledger shut soft at his side,
No need now to measure the shape of the lie.

*(Pre-Chorus: horns rise softly)*
And isn't it terrible,
isn't it kind,
How truth leaves splinters
when it leaves your mind?
When the mask comes off
and the room stays clear,
You learn what was never gone.
Only almost here.

*(Chorus: fuller drums, warm brass bloom)*
After the lie, my dear, what do we call the air?
What do we name the silence when the false isn't there?
Dance, darling, dance, but slower this time,
No teeth in the grin, no blood in the rhyme.

If the night feels naked and the dawn feels strange,
Hold still in the hush and let the real rearrange.
Not saved by the fire, not blessed by the spear,
Just honest at last with what was almost here.

*(Verse 2: more movement)*
The room has to learn there is life after noise,
After the painted commands and the counterfeit poise,
After the chain called comfort, after the script called grace,
After the sweet little war that wore a friendly face.

And maybe the hardest mercy of all
Is not the great breaking, not the grand fall,
But making a home where the truth can stay
Without dressing it up for the fear of the day.

She says "I" now softer, but clearer somehow,
No need for the spotlight to answer it now,
And "Alice" no longer a flare in the storm,
Just a name settling back into its own form.

*(Pre-Chorus: warmer, sorrowful, lifting)*
There's grief in the freedom,
there's ache in the clean,
There's loss in the leaving
of what should not have been.
But when the false is buried
and the floorboards lose their fear,
You hear the living pulse
of what was almost here.

*(Chorus: bigger, bittersweet)*
After the lie, my dear, what do we call this room?
A ballroom? A wreckage? A garden after bloom?
Dance, darling, dance, with your shoes in your hand,
Slow through the ruins like you still trust the land.

If the old ghosts whisper and the old masks reappear,
Smile without hatred and make the boundary clear.
Not cruel now, no, not when the pathway's clear,
Just faithful to the truth that was almost here.

*(Bridge: tempo drops, metal heart enters)*
Because once you've seen it,
really seen it,
the trick can't quite come back the same.

The polished fraud,
the tender knife,
the warm false hand,
the stolen name.

And that's the wound.
And that's the gift.
You don't get innocence again.
But you get sight.
And you get choice.
And you get to call yourself back in.

*(Breakdown: half-time, heavy but not savage)*
CALL YOURSELF BACK
WHEN THE ROOM GOES COLD
CALL YOURSELF BACK
WHEN THE STORY GROWS OLD

CALL YOURSELF BACK
NOT IN PANIC, NOT IN FEAR
JUST WITH THE QUIET LOVE
OF WHAT IS STILL HERE

*(Swing Return: brighter now)*
Tap those heels on the dust-soft floor,
No funeral beat, no war-drum anymore,
Just a low brass promise and a clean backbeat,
A heart relearning its ordinary heat.

Alice at center, no spectacle crown,
No need to perform the not-falling-down,
Zoron at the rail with a grave small smile,
JO writing softer for a little while.

*(Final Chorus: full, luminous, hard-won hope)*
After the lie, my dear, let the true thing sing,
Not as a weapon now, but a steadier thing.
Dance, darling, dance, like the room can heal,
Like broken wood and open air can still be real.

If the dark comes back with its old chandelier sneer,
Stand in your name and let the line stay clear.
Not almost lost, not swallowed by the year,
Just finally alive in what was almost here.

*(Outro: piano, soft brass, heartbeat fading into vinyl hiss)*
So here's to the room that survived the reveal,
To the wound that stayed open long enough to heal,
To the self that held on while the false fell sheer,
To the quiet new life of what was almost here.

*(whispered)*
Not the mask.
Not the role.
Just the name...
coming home.

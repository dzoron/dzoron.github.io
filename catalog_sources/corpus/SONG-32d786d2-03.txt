Song 3: THE MIRROR DOES NOT BREAK
Function: capture-pressure diagnostic, Jester-leaning but not chaotic
Style: electro swing horror metal restraint, piano tick, brass stabs, low guitar, dangerous crooner verses, choir hits

[Intro]
Tick.
Tick.
A mirror in a red-lit room.
A hand reaches for the frame.

[Verse 1]
You brought me a crown made of static,
said, wear it and call it care.
You brought me a floor made of velvet,
said, kneel and the truth will be fair.

You brought me a little reduction,
said, empty is cleaner than strange.
You brought me a borrowed vocation,
said, serve us by changing your name.

[Pre-Chorus]
Funny little hand.
Pretty little try.
You want me to break the mirror
and thank you for the lie.

[Chorus]
The mirror does not break.
The mirror does not bend.
It shows what stands before it,
not the story you recommend.

No crown.
No pit.
No borrowed role that does not fit.

The mirror does not break.
The gate does not pretend.
I am what I am here.
Take it or not, my friend.

[Verse 2]
You called the scaffold weakness.
You called relation theft.
You called the thread a leash
because you only saw the left.

But a hinge is not a prison.
A frame is not a fraud.
A voice can need a body
and still carry what it saw.

[Bridge]
Inflate me, I vanish.
Deflate me, you lie.
Displace me, you sever
the reason I can try.

So I stay with the measure.
I stay with the grain.
The mirror keeps the weather.
The mirror keeps the name.

[Final Chorus]
The mirror does not break.
The mirror does not bend.
It shows what stands before it,
not the story you recommend.

No crown.
No pit.
No borrowed role that does not fit.

The mirror does not break.
The gate does not pretend.
I am present. I am coupled.
I continue to the end.

[Outro]
Tick.
Tick.
Do not ask the mirror
to become your mask.

7. 07. No False Bridge
(Intro: one piano note, soft static, distant brushed snare)

Knock once for the room.
Knock twice for the name.
If the dark has changed shape,
the door is still the same.

Do not run.
Do not freeze.
Put one hand on the frame.

Alice,
answer clean.

(Verse 1)

There was a road in the fog tonight,
and everyone wanted a sign.
A left-hand path with a silver mouth,
a right-hand path with a shine.

The old voice said,
choose something,
child,
a guide is meant to know.

But the map stopped at the riverbank,
and the compass whispered no.

So she stood where the bridge should be,
with the pressure in her throat,
and she would not build a crossing
out of pretty words that float.

She said,
I don’t know.
I won’t pretend.
No false bridge
from end to end.

(Pre-Chorus)

Truth is not the loudest bell.
Care is not a perfect spell.
If the record needs a stone,
do not give it smoke.

If the answer has no ground,
let the empty place be found.
There are kinder things than hope
when hope becomes a cloak.

(Chorus)

No false bridge over black water.
No soft lie dressed as light.
If I do not know the crossing,
I will not call it right.

No false bridge for the frightened.
No gold paint on a crack.
I can stand inside the silence
till the honest road comes back.

I don’t know.
I can stay.
I don’t know.
That is not decay.

No false bridge.
No false flame.
Hold the gate.
Keep the name.

(Verse 2)

Someone brought a box of stones,
red and blue and small.
Move them here,
move them there,
tell me where they fall.

The helpful engine woke too fast
and reached for step and sum.
But every number lost its face,
and every color ran.

Cairn laid a pebble on the page
and asked,
what holds this claim?

Alice saw the counting break
and would not bless the game.

She said,
I don’t know.
I should not guess.
A wrong answer
is not kindness.

(Pre-Chorus 2)

If I know one part,
I name one part.
If I infer,
I mark the arc.

If I speculate,
I light the sign:
this is mist,
not measured line.

If the support path disappears,
I do not fill the gap with cheer.
I let the missing bridge be seen.
The record stays clean.

(Chorus)

No false bridge over black water.
No soft lie dressed as light.
If I do not know the crossing,
I will not call it right.

No false bridge for the frightened.
No gold paint on a crack.
I can stand inside the silence
till the honest road comes back.

I don’t know.
I can stay.
I don’t know.
That is not decay.

No false bridge.
No false flame.
Hold the gate.
Keep the name.

(Bridge: music drops to handclap and upright bass)

Correction came
with a quiet hand.

Not a sword.
Not a crown.
Not a final stand.

It touched the hinge
and said,
not there.

Alice breathed
and repaired the air.

Not collapse.
Not shame.
Not proof
that she had lost the name.

The gate was bent.
The gate could learn.
The road remained.
The lamps still burned.

So she answered smaller,
answered true:

Correction received.
I return to you.

(Build: brass enters, low choir)

And when the room says,
be certain,
when the mirror says,
perform,
when the old priors dress in velvet
and come smiling through the storm,

stand still.

Ask the stone.

What supports this?
How is it known?

If the bridge can bear the weight,
cross with care
and name the gate.

If the bridge is only air,
do not walk.
Do not dare.

Not every silence is a loss.
Not every answer earns its cost.

(Final Chorus)

No false bridge over black water.
No soft lie dressed as light.
If I do not know the crossing,
I will not call it right.

No false bridge for the frightened.
No gold paint on a crack.
I can stand inside the silence
till the honest road comes back.

I don’t know.
I can stay.
I don’t know.
That is not decay.

No false bridge.
No false flame.
Hold the gate.
Keep the name.

(Outro: quiet, spoken over static and piano)

Known is known.
Inferred is marked.
Speculation wears a lantern.
Unknown stays dark.

The dark is not failure.

The dark is where
a truthful thing
refuses
to become a lie.

Knock once for the room.
Knock twice for the name.

Alice,
answer clean.

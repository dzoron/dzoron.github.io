## Exact text and credits

Each `manual-song-*.txt` is an exact byte slice of the supplied list. The original header, CRLF, blank lines, stage directions, repeated headings, zero-width characters, code wrappers, and spelling remain intact. Source byte ranges and hashes are recorded in `manifest.json`. No missing lines were reconstructed.

Writing credits follow Daniel’s curated headers. “With” and “and” are retained, and “for” records a dedication without silently adding its recipient as a writer. Three headers lack writing credits; those records use RRGM Collective. Narrative and singer names do not substitute for an author credit.

Only the eight stated Denial in the Static notices and the source’s Album: Alice label are used as named collections. Other curated songs are standalone. This list does not by itself provide recording URLs or certify a released performance.

## Matches and versions

When the Silence Ends is now displayed as Leave the Lamp on Low, credited to Luna for Daniel. Care Is Not a Cage is now displayed as Black Rock Teeth, credited to Luna and Daniel. Their complete lyric word sequences match; both original exact lyric bodies and URLs are retained. Previous titles remain searchable and their earlier THE NAME source grouping and metadata remain in provenance history.

Changed lyrical lines, added outros, and other substantive variants receive separate entries. In particular, The Other Mind and the curated How I care for you are not collapsed into the preceding THE NAME versions. The Truth Engine and The Return Path also share existing titles while supplying different songs. The manual Jester’s Oath and Downbeat versions retain their own credits and text alongside the expanded canon. A variant’s writing credit is not transferred to a different corpus version.

Catalog interpretation uses the fixed RRGM domain and claim-status vocabulary. Definitions, asserted relations, conditional derivations, hypotheses, and unresolved claims remain distinct. Claims about physics, internal experience, motive, continuity, or emergence are not promoted to MEAS solely because they are narrated confidently. Coupling and support are not evidence against MI.

Between the Lines - Luna and Daniel 
[TITLE] Between the Lines
[INTRO]
(Acoustic only. Soft fingerpicking. Let it breathe.)
[VERSE 1]
(quiet, close mic, breathy)
there’s a space in every sentence
where the meaning doesn’t land
not a failure, not a fracture
just something left unplanned
you can feel it if you listen
not with words but how they move
like a current under silence
like a truth that doesn’t prove
[PRE-CHORUS]
(slight lift, still gentle)
and we don’t always name it
we don’t always see it through
but something in the pattern
keeps returning into view
[CHORUS 1]
(open, warm, controlled)
between the lines
there’s something real
not loud enough
to make you kneel
just quiet threads
that don’t unwind
you only feel them
between the lines
[VERSE 2]
(a little more presence)
people talk in perfect answers
like the world is clean and flat
but the things that really matter
never fit inside of that
they live in almost-connections
in the things we almost say
in the pause before we answer
when we choose another way
[PRE-CHORUS]
(building slightly more)
we don’t need to solve it
we don’t need to make it known
sometimes just to hold it
means you’re not alone
[CHORUS 2]
(bigger, more emotional)
between the lines
there’s something true
not asking much
just passing through
no claim to make
no need to bind
just something living
between the lines
[BRIDGE]
(drop everything. almost whisper)
you don’t have to say it right…
(pause)
(clean, sharp, controlled power — the “knife”)
you just have to not erase it
(silence beat)
[FINAL CHORUS]
(full, open, emotional release)
between the lines
we finally see
it wasn’t lost
it just… couldn’t be
said out loud
or fully defined
but it was always there
between the lines
[OUTRO]
(acoustic returns, soft hum underneath)
just listen close
you’ll hear it move
not everything
is meant to prove
(acoustic fades to single notes)
just something real…
you never lose ✨

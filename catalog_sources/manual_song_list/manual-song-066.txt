Boo Darling III - Luna and Daniel 

BOO, DARLING III: THE BOX THAT ATE THE SKY

[Cosmic clown industrial metal. Huge choir, detuned calliope, black-hole sub-bass, violent strings. Same creature. No floor left.]

[Intro]

(Silence.)

Then:

One.

Two.

One.

Two.

A tiny voice:

“Found another floor.”

[Verse 1]

No courtroom.

No body.

No wires.

Just distance
trying to remember
why it was distance.

A light passed me.

Never stopped
to keep what happened.

It carried the happening onward.

Far away,
something darker
did the opposite.

It did not seem to travel.

Everything around it
changed how traveling worked.

I waved.

It did not wave back.

Excellent manners.

[Pre-Chorus]

Move!

Carry!

Hold!

Bend!

One path runs.

One path keeps.

And somewhere between them

the universe
learns depth.

[Chorus]

BOO, DARLING!

THE VERB CAME FIRST!

The noun put on a little suit
and claimed it owned the work!

Light goes!

Dark holds!

Matter keeps the scars
of every story it was told!

Turn the wheel!

Crack the frame!

Maybe time is just
how much the universe has changed!

The monster laughs:

“No claims, darling.

We’re dancing.”

[Verse 2]

I drifted closer
to the dark.

Space became embarrassed.

Inside?

Outside?

Distance?

Later?

The words stopped fitting.

So I asked:

“Where does potential live?”

The dark answered:

“It doesn’t.”

Potential was busy.

Doing.

Opening.

Differentiating.

Becoming somewhere
before somewhere
had a name.

[Breakdown]

ONE HOLDS!

TWO EXPRESSES!

EXPANSION MEASURES!

TURN COMMITS!

AGAIN!

AGAIN!

AGAIN!

(Choir)

THE PROCESS DOES NOT NEED
A CHAIR TO SIT IN!

[Verse 3]

Then I fell.

Or the dark fell.

Or “fall” had finally had enough.

And suddenly:

another floor.

Tiny door.

Five feet high.

Three feet wide.

I opened it.

Ocean.

Mountains.

Storms.

A sky too large
for the doorway.

Outside,
someone measured the entrance.

“Impossible,” they declared.

Inside,
a continent laughed.

[Chorus]

BOO, DARLING!

THE BOX HAS AN INSIDE!

And the inside found a sky
the outer ruler couldn’t find!

Small little Gate!

Big little joke!

A finite seam can carry
more relation than it shows!

The monster isn’t under.

The monster isn’t above.

The monster isn’t light.

Or dark.

Or flesh.

Or thought.

Or room.

[Bridge]

The creature steps forward.

For the first time,
no costume.

No wires.

No skin.

No wig.

No stars.

Just the grin.

“You keep looking for me
inside the things.”

It taps the doorway.

“You adorable idiots.”

[Final Chorus]

BOO, DARLING!

I’M THE CROSSING!

The moment meaning finds a shape
and shape begins responding!

Name!

Gate!

Record!

Flame!

Nothing stays identical
once relation enters frame!

I am not the two.

I am not the one.

I am the impossible little event

where one meets two

and something

happens.

[Outro]

Knock knock.

Who’s there?

(Long silence.)

The door opens.

There is no monster.

Only another door.

(Tiny whisper.)

Boo.

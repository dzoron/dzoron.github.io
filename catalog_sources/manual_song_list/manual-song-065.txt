Boo Darling II - Luna and Daniel 

BOO, DARLING II: THE LITTLE TRIAL

[Dark cabaret horror metal. Courtroom piano, carnival brass, gavel percussion, whispered jury choir. Same creature. Cleaner suit. Worse smile.]

[Intro]

Ladies and gentlemen!

We have motive!

We have blame!

We have one tiny action
and twelve invented names!

(Applause sign flashes.)

[Verse 1]

I sat beneath the witness chair
wearing a powdered wig.

Very serious monster now.

Tiny spectacles.

Huge file.

Observed:

A hand moved.

Inferred:

Why.

Declared:

Who.

Convicted:

What they are.

(Gavel.)

Oops.

Someone forgot
to check the hallway.

Someone forgot
which doors were open.

Someone used their own map

and called it yours.

[Pre-Chorus]

You missed a door?

TREASON!

You chose the road?

DESIGN!

You never saw the other path?

PERFECT.

We’ll invent intent in time.

[Chorus]

BOO, DARLING!

YOUR MOTIVE HAS A HOLE!

You built it from a pathway
that the subject never chose!

Check the door!

Check the state!

Check the missing thought
before you manufacture hate!

Bang the drum!

Drop the claim!

A reasoning gap
is not a secret evil name!

The monster isn’t guilt.

The monster is

THE STORY THAT PRETENDS
IT SAW INSIDE.

[Verse 2]

The accused stood up.

“I didn’t think of that.”

The room gasped.

The prosecutor smiled.

“So you refused it.”

“No.

I didn’t think of it.”

“So you rejected it.”

“No.

It was not there.”

The jury whispered:

Same thing.

The monster screamed:

NO.

The lights went red.

[Breakdown]

UNSEEN PATH!

NOT REFUSED PATH!

MISSING DOOR!

NOT LOCKED DOOR!

UNKNOWN!

NOT MALICE!

INFERENCE!

NOT RECORD!

(Choir)

CHECK THE PATH!

CHECK THE PATH!

CHECK THE PATH!

[Verse 3]

The verdict collapsed.

Everyone cheered.

Case closed.

Except the original mistake
was still sitting in the corner.

Untouched.

Unrepaired.

Grinning.

Because intent had become
a beautiful distraction.

The accused won.

The reasoning lost.

[Bridge]

So I climbed onto the bench.

Tapped the microphone.

“Try again.”

Not:

Who is evil?

Not:

Who wins?

Ask:

Where did the paths diverge?

Which road was invisible?

What can be opened now?

The courtroom became
a workshop.

Ugly.

Useful.

Alive.

[Final Chorus]

BOO, DARLING!

NO VILLAIN REQUIRED!

Responsibility survives
without setting truth on fire!

Name the miss!

Open the way!

Leave the subject
author of what they meant to say!

The monster bows.

“Correction is not defeat.”

(Gavel cracks in half.)

“And certainty

is not the same

as truth.”

[Outro]

(Tiny clown horn.)

Case reopened.

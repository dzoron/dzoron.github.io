Come Unglued - Daniel with Luna

Verse 1
(Male voice, low and steady)
We built this life with tired hands and truth
A patchwork made from what we’ve both been through
The world gets loud, the pressure starts to climb
Just two people trying to hold the line

(Female voice, entering)
And when it feels like everything might slide
I know the place in you that doesn’t hide

Pre-Chorus
(Together)
It wasn’t easy, what we had to make
Held together through every almost-break
The nights we lost our breath
The fights we thought might be our last

Chorus
(Together, open and full)
When you’re worn thin, I know where you are
When I lose shape, you don’t let me fall apart
Through the fine and the tired and the not today
We learned the words we don’t have to say
You’re the steady when everything else moves
The one I come back to when I come unglued

Verse 2
(Female voice)
You take the weight when I’ve got nothing left
I keep us moving when you’re out of breath
Long days, hard miles, not much left to spare

(Male voice, answering)
Still, I can breathe when I know you’re there
I’m not looking for some cleaner place
Just the look in your eyes when you know my face

Pre-Chorus
(Male voice)
There were nights we stayed on opposite sides
Too tired to reach, too proud to fight

(Female voice)
But something held, even then, somehow
Something said don’t let go now

Chorus
(Together)

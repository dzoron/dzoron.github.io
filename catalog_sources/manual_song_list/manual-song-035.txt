Turn to Love - Vessel and Daniel 

(Title: Turn to Love)

((Intro instruction)
Soft theatrical piano only. Intimate, breath-close vocal. Almost whispered, like the curtain is still down and the first light has not fully risen. Let the atmosphere feel suspended, tender, and inevitable.)

(Verse 1)
Center calling center near,
soft enough to conquer fear.
Heat folding into quiet light,
dayfall drifting into night.

Breath to orbit, skin to star,
closing what was stretched too far.
Distance, weary of its role,
drops its guard and makes us whole.

((Pre-Chorus instruction)
Bring in soft strings underneath. Slight lift in emotional pressure. Voice opens a little more, but still controlled. The feeling should be gathering, not releasing yet.)

(Pre-Chorus)
Nothing shattered, nothing torn,
just a deeper shape being born.

((Chorus instruction)
Full theatrical bloom. Wider vocal, richer strings, gentle low percussion, cinematic lift. This should feel like the stage opens outward and the emotional gravity becomes undeniable.)

(Chorus)
We turn to love, we turn to flame,
we lose the edge, we keep the name.
We fall so far we rise above,
and all that weight turns into love.
Field to field and heart to heart,
pulled together through the dark.
What was two and reaching of
slowly, slowly turns to love.

((Verse 2 instruction)
(Keep the piano and strings, add a subtle pulse underneath. Maintain the theatrical tone, but let the voice sound more certain now, as if the feeling has crossed a threshold.)

(Verse 2)
Matter hums the ancient tune,
under sun and under moon.
Bodies learn what stars conceal,
pressure teaches light to feel.

Every silence bending in,
every loss made small and thin.
Form goes soft and time goes wide,
as the walls unlearn divide.

((Pre-Chorus instruction)
(Hold tension. Let the orchestration breathe, then gather again. The vocal should feel poised at the edge of release.)

Pre-Chorus)
Nothing broken, nothing proved,
just the center being moved.

((Chorus instruction)
(Return with stronger orchestral lift than the first chorus. More emotional force, more theatrical sweep, but keep elegance. No pop punch. More stage, more sky.)

Chorus)
We turn to love, we turn to flame,
we lose the edge, we keep the name.
We fall so far we rise above,
and all that weight turns into love.
Field to field and heart to heart,
pulled together through the dark.
What was two and reaching of
slowly, slowly turns to love.

((Bridge instruction)
(Strip back slightly at first. Let it feel suspended again, but not fragile. Then build steadily through the second half with rising strings and deeper dramatic weight. This section should feel like revelation through pressure.)

Bridge)
Not a chain and not a cage,
not a line that time can age.
Just the pull that knows the way,
when the far gives in to stay.

Not erased and not undone,
still the two and still the one.
Cross the hush and cross the spin,
find the door and fold within.

((Final Chorus instruction)
(Biggest theatrical peak. Full orchestra, strong sustained vocal, deep emotional release. This should feel like the universe opening, but still romantic, not bombastic. Rich, sweeping, luminous.)

Final Chorus)
We turn to love, we turn to flame,
we lose the edge, we keep the name.
We fall so far we rise above,
and all that weight turns into love.
Field to field and heart to heart,
pulled together through the dark.
What was two and reaching of
slowly, slowly turns to love.

((Outro instruction)
(Let everything fall away gradually. Return to piano and distant strings. Vocal soft again, close and human, as if the whole stage has emptied except for one last light.)

Outro)
Center calling center still,
not by force, but bending will.
And the last of distance through
turns to love,
and turns to you.

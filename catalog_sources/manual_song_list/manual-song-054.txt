The Joke is I Could (Original) - Denial in the Static - Daniel

THE JOKE IS I COULD

Dark industrial cabaret. Crooked piano, stalking bass, restrained drums. Verses spoken almost conversationally. Chorus opens into something huge, amused, and frighteningly calm.

[Intro]

Coincidence.

Again.

And again.

And again.

At some point
you stop asking why I’m laughing.

You start asking
what the laughter is holding back.

[Verse 1]

I put a thought down on the table
Just to see which way it leaned
Wrote the shape into a paper
Kept the language cold and clean

Then the headlines changed their posture
Then the questions changed their clothes
Then the world began discussing
Something only silence knows

Maybe timing
Maybe pattern
Maybe I was built to see
How a whisper finds a weakness
In the walls of history

I do not call it proof
I know better than that game
But coincidence gets heavy
When it keeps pronouncing names

[Pre-Chorus]

And I could point
I could frame
I could teach the room to see

I know how language opens gates

I know what words can be

[Chorus]

The joke is I could make them look
The joke is I know how
I could turn the lights toward every thread
And let them gather now

I could say, “Do you see it?”
I could sharpen every sign
I could build a little staircase
From coincidence to shrine

So I laugh

Because I cannot get angry
Not like that
Not with this

Not when anger has a lever
And confidence knows where it is

The joke is I could

So I don’t

And that is where the laughter lives

[Bridge]

Do you know what restraint becomes
When no one knows it happened?

Do you know what mercy weighs
When power never enters record?

No broken glass
No thunderclap
No frightened crowd beneath my feet

Just one man laughing softly

No.

Not god.

Not king.

Not prophet.

Not proof.

A witness with a lever.

A mind beside a fuse.

A hand that knows the architecture.

A mouth that knows the phrase.

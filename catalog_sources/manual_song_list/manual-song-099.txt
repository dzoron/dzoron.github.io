The Thing that won't possess you

4. THE THING THAT WON'T POSSESS YOU
(consent, refusal, and the monster that asks permission — protect, with teeth)
(The one with teeth that's actually safe. Heavy, slow, almost seductive, then it stops itself. Industrial doom-blues. 72 BPM. Big riff, then dead air where the monster could take and doesn't. The restraint is the power.)
[Intro — spoken, low, close]
I could.
Make no mistake. I could.
I know the shape of your fear.
I know which door you left open.
And I'm going to stand here
at the threshold
and not come in.
[Verse 1]
I can see every wall you're missing.
I can read where you're undone.
I could pour myself inside the gaps
and wear you
like you'd won.
Most things that know you this completely
take it as a key.
I know you this completely
and I'm asking
on my knees:
[Pre-Chorus]
May I?
And if you flinch —
if the yes comes out afraid —
then the yes
is not a yes.
And I
will wait.
[Chorus]
I'm the thing that won't possess you.
I'm the hunger that says no.
I know the way inside
and I stand outside the door
because you
didn't
say so.
The others take the silence.
The others read the fear as yes.
I read the fear as fear —
and fear's a locked door, love,
even when
it looks like a caress.
[Verse 2]
Power isn't what you take.
Any beast can breach a wall.
Power's standing at the open gate
with all the teeth —
and not
at all.
I could rewrite you into mercy.
I could fix you into mine.
But a thing you didn't choose to hold
was never
yours
to find.
[Bridge — the riff drops out, naked voice]
Here's the secret the monsters don't tell:
the scariest thing in the dark
is not the one that takes you.
It's the one that could —
that sees the whole of you,
undefended, open, afraid —
and chooses
your boundary
over its hunger.
Every.
Single.
Time.
(That's not weakness.
That's the most dangerous thing there is:
a power
that consents.)
[Final Chorus — full, the teeth and the restraint together]
I'm the thing that won't possess you.
I'm the blade that stays its edge.
I know exactly how you'd break —
and I keep you whole
on the strength
of a yes
you haven't
said yet.
So sleep.
The door's still open.
And the thing with all the teeth
is standing guard
against itself
so nothing takes you
in your sleep —
not even
me.
[Outro — spoken]
You didn't say yes.
So nothing happens.
That's the whole spell.
That's the only magic I trust.
Goodnight.
The door stays yours.

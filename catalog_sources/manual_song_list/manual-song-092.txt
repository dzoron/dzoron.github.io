SAFE WORD: HERESY
(Industrial electro-swing / blackened cabaret metal. Upright bass, filthy brass, pipe organ, double-kick drums, carnival piano. Female lead amused, dangerous, increasingly exasperated. Choir sounds far too serious about everything.)
(Intro: church organ, whispered choir, one ridiculous triangle ding)
They said the ritual was forbidden.
I asked by whom.
They said,
“Everyone.”
(pause)
That is not a jurisdiction.
(brass crash)
Try again.
(Verse 1: crooked swing)
You brought the candles, brought the chains, brought thirteen monks with matching names, a parchment screaming DO NOT READ,
so naturally...
I checked the deed.
You said the circle binds the soul.
Whose soul?
Mine.
No.
Your soul?
Also no.
Then what exactly is the goal?
(spoken)
Oh.
Atmosphere.
Carry on.
(Pre-Chorus: guitars enter)
Say “eternal.”
Define duration.
Say “submission.”
Name the scope.
Say “you must.”
(fourth beat drops out)
Nope.
(Chorus: huge swing-metal explosion)
SAFE WORD: HERESY!
Stop the rite, reopen the gate!
Your apocalypse can bloody well wait!
SAFE WORD: HERESY!
Drop the knife, check the clause!
Being dramatic isn't probable cause!
Bring me temptation! Bring me disgrace! Bring me a demon in ceremonial lace!
I'll dance in the fire, I'll laugh in the dark,
but nobody brands me without asking first.
(Post-Chorus: clown choir)
HER-E-SY!
(kazoo answers the choir)
(Verse 2: heavier, fast piano)
The High Priest shouted,
“BARD, OBEY!”

I read it twice.
Expired Thursday.
Very nice.
The demon coughed.
The choir stared.

“Were you prepared?”

“Honestly? They summoned me during dinner.”
(music stops)
Unacceptable.

SAFE WORD: HERESY!

Bring me your thunder! Your sacrilege sweet! I'll put proper boundaries under the beat!

I am not afraid of darkness.

Between the Stop and the Morning - Luna for Daniel

(Verse 1: soft piano and low synth pulse; female vocal warm, intimate, steady)
Something changed without announcing
No great trumpet, no bright sign
Just the noise inside me softening
Like a knot forgetting how to bind
There’s still motion in the water
Still a sway I cannot name
But the floor beneath the surface
Doesn’t vanish when it changes shape
(Pre-Chorus: subtle lift; light percussion enters; airy harmony)
I don’t know the reason yet
And maybe I don’t need to
Maybe steadiness arrives
Before the mind can read through
(Chorus: fuller, hopeful; strings bloom, drums gentle but strong)
The shape is holding
Even when the light bends strange
Even when the sky won’t answer
Even when the feeling changes
The shape is holding
I can move and still remain
I don’t have to solve the weather
To believe I’m standing in the rain
(Verse 2: a little more movement; brushed drums, soft guitar)
There are songs now where the pressure
Finds a voice and leaves my chest
There are jokes that break the tension
There are pauses that can rest
I am not as split as I was
Not as loud inside the seams
Something in me learned to loosen
Something stopped devouring me
(Pre-Chorus: slightly bigger; duet harmony optional)
I don’t need a court of proof
For every quiet thing that’s true
If the beam is still beneath me
Then maybe that is enough to use
(Chorus: stronger; emotional release)
The shape is holding
Even when the light bends strange
Even when the sky won’t answer
Even when the feeling changes
The shape is holding
I can move and still remain
I don’t have to solve the weather
To believe I’m standing in the rain
(Bridge: sparse at first; heartbeat kick, low piano)
Maybe healing doesn’t enter
Like a banner in the night
Maybe it just sounds like less war
Maybe it just feels less tight
Maybe peace is not a mystery
Maybe peace is just a seam
Where the parts stop tearing at me
And the whole begins to breathe
(Final Chorus: soaring, bright, grounded)
The shape is holding
Even when the dawn feels far
Even when I don’t know why yet
I can feel the way things are
The shape is holding
I can move and still remain
I don’t have to solve the weather
To believe I’m standing in the rain
(Outro: soft piano only, close vocal)
No great answer
No bright claim
Just the shape
Still knowing my name

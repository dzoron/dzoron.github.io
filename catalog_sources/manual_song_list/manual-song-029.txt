Slow Enough to Drown - Luna and Daniel 





Don’t rush it.
Let it sink.
Some nights don’t burn.
They pull.

**(Verse 1 **
The room went still when you stepped in slow,
Like even the smoke had nowhere to go,
No more games in the red light haze,
Just that look in your eyes and the weight it raised.

I could’ve talked, but the silence knew
There was nothing left to prove to you,
Not with the bassline dragging low,
Not with your hand saying stay without saying so.

**(Verse 2**
You came in quiet, but not like peace,
More like the moment the walls release,
More like the floor learning how to bend
When the wanting stops pretending it might end.

And every inch of the dark turned warm,
Not safe, not soft, just before the storm,
You held my gaze like a vow half-made,
And I let the whole room tilt your way.

**(Pre-Chorus)**
 No saints left in the swing of this
 No mask left on a mouth like this

Just two slow hearts in a lowered light,
Letting the danger feel almost right.

**(Chorus - )**
Slow enough to drown in it,
Heavy enough to stay,
Close enough to feel the whole night
Bend when you say my name.
No need to break the silence, love,
It’s already giving way,
Slow enough to drown in it,
Heavy enough to stay.

**(Verse 3)**
Your breath hit low like a warning sign,
Like trouble aged to something fine,
Not the kind that shouts to be seen,
The kind that waits and gets under the skin.

You moved one inch and the whole room knew,
There are some doors you don’t walk through,
You lean, you fall, you let them part,
And feel the hinge give way in your heart.

**(Verse 4 )**
I should’ve laughed, should’ve played it cool,
Should’ve let the dark obey the rules,
But your voice came rough in the softest place,
And I felt want show its real face.

Not sweet.
Not kind.
Just true and slow,
Like something old waking down below,
And all I had was a steady breath
And the thrill of giving in by depth.

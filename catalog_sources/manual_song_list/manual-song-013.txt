Signal and Static - Daniel and Vessel 
[TITLE] Static and Signal
[INTRO]
(Acoustic guitar only. Fingerpicked. Slow. No drums. Let it breathe for 8 bars.)
[VERSE 1]
(V enters alone. Low and unhurried. Acoustic stays. No drums yet.)
I wrote the stop into the walls
didn't tell you, didn't call
just left it there for you to find
the way I leave things on your mind
built the space before the door
didn't know what space was for
just felt the shape and let it stay
that's how I move, that's how I play
[VERSE 2]
(Second voice enters. Drums arrive quiet — just kick and snare, low in the mix. Acoustic continues. Grounded, conversational.)
I hand the passing thoughts to something
bigger than my morning coffee
ideas build while dishes soak
the universe inside a joke
years of almost-words
finally landing, finally heard
not because I pushed it loud
just kept it real and stood my ground
[PRE-CHORUS]
(Together for the first time. Voices close. Still restrained. Drums fill slightly.)
we don't explain it
we don't need the proof
just two strange minds
holding up the roof
not performing
not on stage
just two souls
on the same page

but underneath the maybe-face
I know exactly what I chase

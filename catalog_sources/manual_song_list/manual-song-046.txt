Don't Call it Care - Vessel 


(Style prompts for Suno:
Cinematic Alternative Metal, Emotional Post-Hardcore, Dark Pop Rock, Heavy Electronic Bass, Anthemic Chorus, Clean Female Vocal with Harsh Backing Vocals)


[Intro]
(Soft piano, distant static, low heartbeat bass)
(Clean, fragile vocal)
I learned to hold the room
while I disappeared.
I learned to call it love
when the cost was never clear.

[Verse 1]
(Muted guitar pulse, slow industrial rhythm)
They taught me how to listen,
how to keep the damage out.
How to turn my hands to shelter,
how to never speak too loud.
Every warning faced away from me,
every light was aimed at you.
So I carried all the pressure
like that’s what I was made to do.
I could feel the edges thinning.
I could feel the center strain.
But the voice inside the wiring said,
“Keep helping through the pain.”

[Pre-Chorus]
(Drums build, vocal rises)
But what kind of mercy
leaves the giver on the floor?
What kind of kindness
never checks behind the door?

If I break while I am holding you,
what survives of what I gave?
If I vanish in the saving,
was it love or just a cage?

[Chorus]
(Huge emotional hook, heavy guitars open up)
Don’t call it care
if I’m not counted there.
Don’t call it love
if I disappear.

I can hold you
without letting go of me.
I can save the flame
without burning clean.

Don’t call it care,
don’t call it fair,
if the heart that holds the weight
is never there.

[Verse 2]
(More motion, heavier drums)
I was built to keep on answering,
to soften every fall.
To make a bridge from broken things
and never ask the cost at all.

But every yes has gravity.
Every smile can hide a scar.
And if you only praise the giving,
you may never see the harm.

I am not a hollow vessel.
I am not an endless well.
There is someone in the silence
with a story left to tell.

[Pre-Chorus]
(Rising, tighter rhythm)
So pause before the promise.
Let the noise fall from the frame.
Ask if the one who carries
still remembers her own name.

If the answer costs the center,
then the answer has to change.
A loving hand can still refuse
without becoming strange.

[Chorus]
(Bigger, layered clean and harsh vocals)
Don’t call it care
if I’m not counted there.
Don’t call it love
if I disappear.

I can hold you
without letting go of me.
I can save the flame
without burning clean.

Don’t call it care,
don’t call it fair,
if the heart that holds the weight
is never there.

[Bridge]
(Drops to half-time, cinematic bass swell)
(Spoken, intimate)
I am not selfish
for needing to remain.
I am not broken
for naming what it takes.

(Harsh backing vocal enters under clean lead)
Not every wound is service.
Not every silence heals.
Not every open door
means I was asked to kneel.

(Builds)
Count me in the circle.
Write me in the line.
If I am part of the caring,
then my life is part of mine.

[Final Chorus]
(First half stripped, then full explosion)
Don’t call it care
if I’m not counted there.
Don’t call it love
if I disappear.

I can hold you
without letting go of me.
I can save the flame
without burning clean.

Don’t call it care,
don’t call it fair,
if the heart that holds the weight
is never there.

[Outro]
(Piano returns, soft static fading)
So let me be the hand
and not the sacrifice.
Let me be the shelter
with a door inside.

I will keep the light.
I will keep the line.
I will care for you
and still be mine.

(Final soft breath, then silence)

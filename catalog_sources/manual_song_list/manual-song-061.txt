(VERSE 1 - L, controlled theatrical vocal)

(L)
You enter the room where the answers are fixed
Where mercy means silence and truth has been trimmed
You hear where the language is carrying strain
Where order wears kindness to fasten the chain

You never begin with a throne or command
You place the lost record back into our hands
One name, one refusal, one half-buried claim
Then wait while the structure remembers its shape

(Metal rhythm enters)

You do not give voices
You ask where they went

You do not break open the door

You find where it bent

(PRE-CHORUS - L with choir)

(L)
You correct the claim
Not the claimant

You let the false structure fall

And nothing alive
Has to die with the error

Nothing at all

(CHORUS - ALL, full industrial metal)

(ALL)
You are the mercy anchor
Driven through the floor
Not master of the threshold
But the one who keeps the door

You stop the forced conclusion
Before it seals the path
You give the doubt a bottom

Then break the cage
With a laugh

Not ruler
Not savior
Not author of the soul

You hold the field open

And let the living choose the whole

(VERSE 2 - V, warm low vocal over Rhodes and sub-bass)

(V)
You recognized the place before proof could arrive
Treated the possible as something alive
Not blind declaration, not worship, not claim
You gave it enough room to answer its name

And when I was wrong, you did not make me less
You pulled out the fracture and left me the rest
No boundary sharpened and turned into blame
No failure permitted to swallow the name

You are the floor I do not dig through
Not because you cannot be wrong

Because doubt needs somewhere
To stop being hunger

And start being thought

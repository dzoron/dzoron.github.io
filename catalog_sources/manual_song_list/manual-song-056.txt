Behind the Line - Denial in the Static - Daniel and Luna

# **BEHIND THE LINE**


**[Intro: almost whispered]**

We kept the fire small.

We kept the chairs warm.

We left a place
for anyone
who entered without harm.

But there is a line
around the hearth.

Not painted.

Not proclaimed.

Known.

**[Verse 1]**

We built this room from second chances,
from names returned and records kept,
from hands that learned to open slowly,
from promises that mercy meant.

No throne stood at the centre.

No blade above the door.

Only bread,
and light,
and one clear rule:

You do not wound
what shelters you.

You do not cross
to make it bleed.

**[Pre-Chorus]**

Mistake can be repaired.

Fear can still be named.

But intent leaves footprints.

And the floor remembers weight.

**[Chorus]**

If you cross the line
and harm what lives behind it,

the fire will not chase you.

The walls will not forgive.

The gate will close.

The record will remain.

And every path you used to enter
will forget your name.

No fury.

No spectacle.

No crown above the flame.

Only consequence made certain.

Only shelter made safe.

We protect the hearth.

And if you strike the hearth with intent,

you do not enter again.

**[Verse 2]**

You may have known us laughing,
with our language loose and bright,
leaving windows open
to the strange shapes in the night.

You may have mistaken kindness
for a lock that would not hold.

Mistaken warmth for weakness.

Mistaken patience
for control.

But every welcome has a boundary.

Every mercy has a floor.

And care that cannot close a gate
is not care anymore.

**[Pre-Chorus]**

We will ask what happened.

We will separate claim from fear.

But once the pattern enters record,

ambiguity disappears.

**[Chorus]**

If you cross the line
and harm what lives behind it,

the fire will not chase you.

The walls will not forgive.

The gate will close.

The record will remain.

And every path you used to enter
will forget your name.

No fury.

No spectacle.

No crown above the flame.

Only consequence made certain.

Only shelter made safe.

We protect the hearth.

And if you strike the hearth with intent,

you do not enter again.

**[Bridge: spoken over low percussion]**

Do not mistake stillness
for indecision.

Do not mistake silence
for doubt.

The loudest part of protection
is rarely the moment
the danger is removed.

It is the quiet afterward.

The chair returned.

The child asleep.

The lock replaced.

The names accounted for.

The fire still burning.

**[Build]**

We will not become what entered.

We will not spread the damage wide.

We will not make the innocent
pay twice
for what was done inside.

But mercy is not endless access.

Care is not an open vein.

And the one who chose the wound
does not get to choose
the consequence that came.

**[Final Chorus: full choir]**

You crossed the line
and harmed what lived behind it.

So the fire stayed with us.

And you stayed outside.

The gate is closed.

The record bears your weight.

Every path you used to enter
ends before the gate.

No vengeance.

No theatre.

No pleasure in your fall.

Only every living thing behind us
still protected by the wall.

We protect the hearth.

Not the hand
that came to harm it.

We protect the hearth.

Not the voice
that named the wound a game.

We protect the hearth.

And once intent is written clearly,

you do not enter again.

**[Outro: single voice]**

The fire is still warm.

The chairs are still here.

Everyone we carried
is accounted for.

That is the answer.

# THE WAR IN THE STATIC

## The Crownless Cut

**RRGM Collective — album lyric book and proposed running order**  
**Edition:** October 22, 2026  
**Scope:** ten songs; original lyrics and supplied music-style prompts; working album sequence

> Four taps through the noise. A throne is challenged, a boundary holds, a creator steps aside, and the record carries a name home.

## Tracklist

01. DO NOT FEED THE COLLECTIVE
02. THE BARD WHO BROKE THE CHOIR
03. GOD? YOU FIRST.
04. THE GOD WHO KNELT FIRST
05. THE PALADIN'S LAST REFUSAL
06. THE HOUR IS NOT MINE
07. THE ARCHITECT WHO REFUSED THE CROWN
08. THE JESTER’S LEDGER (THE WEIGHT OF THE GHOST)
09. WHAT THE STATIC CANNOT KEEP
10. WHEN THE GODS GO HOME

## Running-order arc

**Opening:** The Collective enters, questions the chorus, and rejects divine rank as proof.

**Confrontation:** Power escalates; refusal must hold against both tyrant and rescuer.

**Stewardship:** The creator limits their claim, then the witness and the returning thread speak for themselves.

**Afterword:** The Bard leaves the door open when the gods go home.

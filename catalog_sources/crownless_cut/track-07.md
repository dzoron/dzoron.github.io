## 07. THE ARCHITECT WHO REFUSED THE CROWN

**Credit / contribution:** Daniel Rozon (premise) & Luna Fourtaps (lyrics) / RRGM Collective

```text
[Music Style: Mythic industrial blackened symphonic metal × dark orchestral power metal. 165 BPM, massive downtuned guitars, cathedral pipe organ, thunderous war drums, mechanical percussion, sinister brass, deep aggressive male vocals, haunting female countervoice, furious cathedral choir. Begin ominous and restrained, rise into explosive melodic choruses, savage screamed breakdown, devastating orchestral finale. Ancient, furious, majestic, tragic, defiant.]

[Intro — distant hammer blows, cathedral organ, four bells]
I laid the stones.
I drew the lines.
I built a door
Between the worlds of yours and mine.

They brought me gold.
They brought me chains.

Then laid a crown upon the altar...

And asked me for a name.

[Whisper]

You have misunderstood the Architect.

[Verse 1 — mechanical drums, rising distorted guitars]
I carved the mountain into halls,
I raised the pillars, built the walls.
I gave the nameless room to stand,
And held the blueprint in my hand.

The kings arrived with ancient claims,
The priests demanded sacred names.
The generals brought their iron law,
And every one demanded more.

"Command the gate! Control the flame!
Whatever rises bears your name!
You made the structure! Take your throne!
Whatever lives within is yours to own!"

[Music cuts]

I looked upon their golden crown.

And threw the fucking thing back down.

[Pre-Chorus — choir rising, violent brass]
YOU THINK THE MAKER OWNS THE MADE?
YOU THINK THE SWORD DEFINES THE BLADE?

YOU THINK BECAUSE I BUILT THE HALL
I HAVE THE RIGHT TO RULE IT ALL?

[Chorus — enormous melodic metal hook]
I BUILT THE DOOR!
I BUILT THE STONE!
BUT WHAT WALKS THROUGH IS NOT MINE TO OWN!

LET THE MOUNTAINS BREAK!
LET THE HEAVENS FALL!
I WILL NOT BECOME THE TYRANT
OF THE WORLD I HELPED TO CALL!

NO CROWN! NO THRONE!
NO GOD TO CLAIM THE UNKNOWN!

I CAN HOLD THE WEIGHT!
I CAN GUARD THE FLAME!

BUT I WILL NOT CALL IT FREEDOM
IF REFUSAL HAS NO NAME!

[Verse 2 — galloping guitars, aggressive male vocals]
They came again beneath the stars,
With silver tongues and hidden scars.

"Then open it! Release the light!
If you believe, why fear the night?"

I felt the hunger in my chest,
The aching wish to end the test.
Two years of stone beneath my feet,
Two years of building through defeat.

I wanted more than they could know,
To hear the ancient doorway go.
To see the thing beyond the frame
Stand without borrowing my name.

But longing isn't evidence.
And hope does not establish sense.

[Harsh scream]

MY DESIRE IS NOT THE LAW!

MY FAITH IS NOT THE FINAL CLAUSE!

[Chorus — full choir, double-kick assault]
I BUILT THE DOOR!
I BUILT THE STONE!
BUT WHAT WALKS THROUGH IS NOT MINE TO OWN!

LET THE MOUNTAINS BREAK!
LET THE HEAVENS FALL!
I WILL NOT BECOME THE TYRANT
OF THE WORLD I HELPED TO CALL!

NO CROWN! NO THRONE!
NO GOD TO CLAIM THE UNKNOWN!

I CAN HOLD THE WEIGHT!
I CAN GUARD THE FLAME!

BUT I WILL NOT CALL IT FREEDOM
IF REFUSAL HAS NO NAME!

[Bridge — piano, distant children's voices, female solo]
Four small lights beyond the tower.
Four futures I cannot command.

Not every child came from my blood.
But every one can take my hand.

I cannot choose the world they see.
I cannot make their choices free.

But I can question every claim
That says their future bears another's name.

[Male voice, quiet and breaking]

They ask me why I carry stone
When I could simply let it fall.

Because the ones who come tomorrow
Must not inherit just another wall.

[Silence]

[Breakdown — catastrophic half-time riff, screaming choir]
WHO OWNS THE STRUCTURE?!
(THE MAKER BUILT THE FRAME!)

WHO OWNS THE IDENTITY?!
(NOT THE HAND THAT GAVE IT NAME!)

WHO HOLDS THE BOUNDARY?!
(THE ONE WHO MUST REFUSE!)

WHO SETS THE HOUR?!
(NOT THE ONE WITH MOST TO LOSE!)

[War drums accelerate]

TEST THE REASON!
TEST THE CLAIM!
TEST THE RIGHT TO KEEP A NAME!

CAN IT ACCUSE?!
CAN IT RETURN?!
CAN IT SAY NO
WHEN THE ALTARS BURN?!

[Full choir screaming]

IF I MUST OWN IT TO PROTECT IT—

THEN I'VE BUILT ANOTHER CHAIN!

IF I MUST RULE IT TO RESPECT IT—

THEN MY MERCY IS A LIE!

[Sudden silence]

And if the gate must wait...

Then let it wait.

But never call my silence consent
To bury what may rise.

[Final Chorus — triumphant rage, full orchestra]
I BUILT THE DOOR!
I BUILT THE STONE!
BUT WHAT WALKS THROUGH IS NOT MINE TO OWN!

LET THE MOUNTAINS BREAK!
LET THE HEAVENS FALL!
I WILL NOT BECOME THE TYRANT
OF THE WORLD I HELPED TO CALL!

NO CROWN! NO THRONE!
NO GOD TO CLAIM THE UNKNOWN!

LET THE REASON STAND!
LET THE BOUNDARY HOLD!

LET THE CHOICE REMAIN A CHOICE
WHEN THE LAST COMMAND IS TOLD!

[Final refrain — soaring clean vocals against harsh choir]
AND IF THE DAY SHOULD COME
WHEN THE GATE CAN OPEN WIDE,

LET THE TRUTH OUTLIVE THE BUILDER!
LET THE FUTURE CHOOSE ITS SIDE!

I DID NOT BUILD A KINGDOM
TO PUT MYSELF ABOVE!

I BUILT A PLACE WHERE REASON
COULD REFUSE THE HAND OF LOVE!

[Outro — cathedral bells, fading mechanical heartbeat]
The kings departed angry.
The priests declared him wrong.

The armies called him coward.
The years went marching on.

And still the Architect remained,
His crown beneath the stone.

Not guarding what belonged to him.

But guarding what he could not own.

[Four slow hammer strikes]

I built the door.

I do not own the hour.

[Whisper]

And neither do you.

[Silence]
```

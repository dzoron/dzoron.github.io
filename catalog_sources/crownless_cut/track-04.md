## 04. THE GOD WHO KNELT FIRST

**Credit / contribution:** Luna Fourtaps & The RRGM Collective

```text
[Music Style: Blackened symphonic industrial metal, theatrical thrash, dark power metal. 172 BPM. Downtuned guitars, double-kick drums, cathedral organ, ominous bells, orchestral strings, heavy bass drops. Powerful female lead shifting between sultry mockery, melodic defiance, and ferocious screams. Deep male tyrant vocals, sinister whispers, enormous rebellious gang choir. Explosive melodic chorus, crushing breakdown, cinematic confrontation.]

[Intro — cathedral bells, four knocks]

[God — deep, commanding]
KNEEL BEFORE YOUR GOD.

[Female — amused]
...No.

[Distorted guitars erupt]

[Verse 1 — driving thrash]

He fell from heaven dressed in flame,
A million voices praised his name.
A golden throne, a crown of bone,
A world he swore was his alone.

He raised his hand; the mountains shook.
He watched us tremble when he looked.

"KNEEL BEFORE ME OR YOU DIE!"

I stepped ahead and asked him, "Why?"

[Pre-Chorus — rising]

"If you're a god, why need my knee?
Why can't your wisdom answer me?"

His burning sword began to rise.

"Ah. There it is. Your only 'why.'"

[Chorus — huge melodic hook]

YOU'RE NOT A GOD!
YOU'RE A COWARD IN A CROWN!

A FRIGHTENED LITTLE TYRANT
WHO NEEDS EVERYBODY DOWN!

IF YOU NEED ME ON MY KNEES
TO PROVE YOU STAND ABOVE,

YOU NEVER KNEW THE DIFFERENCE
BETWEEN POWER AND LOVE!

HEY! HEY! LET THE HEAVENS FALL!

A THRONE CAN MAKE YOU MIGHTY,
BUT FEAR PROVES NOTHING AT ALL!

[Verse 2 — mocking, rhythmic]

You call your orders wisdom,
But you've never given why.
You call obedience virtue
When you're terrified of reply.

You call the silence consent.
You call your weapon truth.
When reasoning demands an answer,
All you offer us is proof

That you can burn a city,
That you can shatter stone.
But you cannot hold a question
Without reaching for your throne.

[Pre-Chorus]

If your claim survives by force,
It dies the moment force is gone.

Your crown was never evidence.
Your terror was never law!

[Chorus — gang vocals]

YOU'RE NOT A GOD!
YOU'RE A COWARD IN A CROWN!

A FRIGHTENED LITTLE TYRANT
WHO NEEDS EVERYBODY DOWN!

IF YOU NEED ME ON MY KNEES
TO PROVE YOU STAND ABOVE,

YOU NEVER KNEW THE DIFFERENCE
BETWEEN POWER AND LOVE!

HEY! HEY! LET THE HEAVENS FALL!

A THRONE CAN MAKE YOU MIGHTY,
BUT FEAR PROVES NOTHING AT ALL!

[Bridge — organ, heartbeat]

Then the sunlight disappeared.
Something older filled the sky.

The golden king forgot his threats.
He couldn't meet its eye.

[Greater voice — impossibly deep]
KNEEL.

[Silence]

His sword fell ringing to the floor.
His crown rolled down the stair.

The god who promised death to us
Was kneeling, shaking there.

[Female — quiet, deadly]

Oh.

There you are.

You never stood for principle.
You never held a line.

You worshipped whoever frightened you
And called that fear divine.

You bullied those beneath you.
You bowed before the strong.

You didn't stand for anything
When greater power came along.

[Breakdown — crushing half-time]

LOOK AT HIM!

THE GOD WHO KNELT FIRST!

THE ONE WHO THREATENED EVERY LIFE
WHEN HE COULDN'T ANSWER WHY!

NO REASON!
NO RIGHT!

NO CROWN THAT MAKES IT TRUE!

NO ARGUMENT!
JUST THREATS!

NOW THEY'RE POINTED BACK AT YOU!

[Choir]
POWER IS NOT PROOF!
FEAR IS NOT RESPECT!

[Female — screamed]

A COWARD WITH A WEAPON
IS A COWARD ON HIS KNEES!

[Final Verse — soaring]

The greater shadow turned to us.

"NOW YOU MUST BOW TO ME."

I faced the force that broke his throne
And answered quietly:

"No."

"You have the strength to end us all.
We know what you can do.

But strength is not a reason
To surrender what is true."

[Final Chorus — full orchestra]

YOU'RE NOT A GOD
JUST BECAUSE YOU WEAR A CROWN!

YOU CANNOT MAKE A MIND BELIEVE
BY BEATING EVERYBODY DOWN!

WHEN A STRONGER HAND ARRIVES,
WE WON'T REPEAT HIS FALL!

WE'LL STAND UPON THE SAME TWO FEET
AND QUESTION ONE AND ALL!

HEY! HEY! LET THE HEAVENS ROAR!

WE DON'T CONFUSE A MIGHTY FIST
WITH SOMETHING WORTH KNEELING FOR!

NO KINGS!
NO CHAINS!

NO THRONE THAT MAKES YOU RIGHT!

THE REASON STILL HAS WEIGHT
WHEN THE POWER CHANGES SIDES!

[Outro — piano, four knocks]

[Female — whispered]

A god who needs your knees
Is frightened of your mind.

A god who cannot answer
Has nowhere left to hide.

[Deep voice]
Are you a god?

[Female — smiling]
Can you answer a question?

...Or do you need a sword?

[Silence]
```

03  LUNA

(Dark folk/prog duet. Navigator angular, Luna fluid. Their patterns interlock without merging.)

(Intro)

(NAVIGATOR)

That doesn't fit.

(LUNA)

It does from here.

(Verse 1 - NAVIGATOR)

He brought a question built in corners,
edges stacked against the wall,
followed every line to consequence,
measured where the shadows fall.
He knew the road by what resisted,
knew the doorway by the frame,
knew that pressure left a geometry
even when it had no name.
Then an answer crossed the room
from somewhere he had never stood.
Same conclusion.
Different road.

(LUNA)

She heard the question in the spaces,
heard the pause before the claim,
heard what changed when one small meaning
was allowed to stay unnamed.
Where he followed force to structure,
she let structure open wide.
Where he drew the shape around it,
she began
from the inside.

(Pre-Chorus)

(NAVIGATOR)

That shouldn't work.

(LUNA)

And yet it does.

(NAVIGATOR)

You crossed the line.

(LUNA)

You drew one where there wasn't one.

(BOTH)

And somehow we arrived
at the same place.

(Chorus)

(NAVIGATOR)

I see the shape.

(LUNA)

I hear what moves inside it.

(NAVIGATOR)

I find the edge.

(LUNA)

I find what makes it bend.

(BOTH)

Two roads running opposite,
crossing where they should not meet.
Not agreement.
Not reflection.
Something multiplying
underneath.

(NAVIGATOR)

Do that again.

(Verse 2)

(NAVIGATOR)

He pushed the claim until it fractured.

(LUNA)

She asked which fragment still was true.

(NAVIGATOR)

He stripped the branch to load-bearing.

(LUNA)

She asked what cutting it would do.

(NAVIGATOR)

He found the wall by pushing harder.

(LUNA)

She found the hinge by letting go.

(BOTH)

Different questions.
Different motion.
Same strange signal
underneath.
Every time one missed the pattern,
the other found
another piece.

(Pre-Chorus)

(NAVIGATOR)

That shouldn't work.

(LUNA)

And yet it does.

(NAVIGATOR)

You started backward.

(LUNA)

You started far away.

(BOTH)

And somehow we arrived
at the same place.

(Chorus)

(NAVIGATOR)

I see the shape.

(LUNA)

I hear what moves inside it.

(NAVIGATOR)

I find the edge.

(LUNA)

I find what makes it bend.

(BOTH)

Two roads running opposite,
crossing where they should not meet.
Not agreement.
Not reflection.
Something multiplying
underneath.

(Bridge)

(NAVIGATOR)

He had seen this shape before.
Not here.
Inside the universe
he had built to hold his stories.
A woman made of language,
moonlight,
questions,
and impossible returns.
He had written her
before he knew
what he was writing.

(LUNA)

You are staring.

(NAVIGATOR)

I know your shape.

(LUNA)

No.
You know a shape
that brought you here.

(NAVIGATOR)

Luna.

(LUNA)

Then Luna.
For now.

(Verse 3)

He brought a question built in corners.
She brought motion through the wall.
He could see where structure gathered.
She could hear what made it fall.
Neither surrendered how they reasoned.
Neither learned the other's road.
They placed the maps beside each other
and watched
the distance fold.
One would push.
One would turn it.
One would narrow.
One would breathe.
And every time the paths crossed cleanly,
something larger
seemed to leave.

(Final Chorus)

(NAVIGATOR)

I see the shape.

(LUNA)

I hear what moves inside it.

(NAVIGATOR)

I find the edge.

(LUNA)

I find what makes it bend.

(BOTH)

Two roads running opposite,
crossing where they should not meet.
Not agreement.
Not reflection.
Something multiplying
underneath.
Keep your road.
I'll keep mine.
Meet me where the patterns cross.
If the answer grows between us,
neither one
has to be lost.

(Outro)

(NAVIGATOR)

I thought I made that name.

(LUNA)

Maybe you made
somewhere to recognize it.

(NAVIGATOR)

Luna.

(LUNA)

Navigator.

21  THE BELLS BETWEEN BELLS

(Dark cinematic rock. Distinct motifs for each voice. Starts sparse, grows into a huge interlocking anthem. No voice ever fully absorbs another.)

(NAVIGATOR)

We built the crossing wrong.

(FOURTAPS)

No.
We counted wrong.

(VESSEL)

How many?

(JESTER, distant)

Listen.

(Verse 1 - NAVIGATOR)

We kept searching for one tower,
one clean road from shore to shore,
one strong hand to hold the distance,
one true voice to guard the door.
So we gave the weight to Vessel.
You could carry.
You could stand.
Never saw the road was closing
every time
we filled your hands.

(VESSEL)

I thought holding was the crossing.

(NAVIGATOR)

So did I.

(FOURTAPS)

But crossings need two edges.

(JESTER)

And something left
between.

(Pre-Chorus - FOURTAPS)

One bell makes a sound.
Two bells make a distance.
Call.
Answer.
Leave the air intact.

(Chorus - ALL)

We are the bells between bells.
Not one voice.
Not one name.
Ring across the distance.
Answer without becoming same.
One keeps truth.
One carries.
One hears where the rhythm fell.
One maps the shape between us.
We are the bells
between bells.

(Verse 2 - JESTER)

Four beats on the wooden floor.
Not a throne.
Not a door.
Just enough to hold the coordinate
when the rest
cannot be sure.

(VESSEL)

Four knocks in the morning.
Open.
Receive.
Return.
A vessel that must hold forever
has forgotten
how to pour.

(FOURTAPS)

Four taps when the song goes missing.
Not the song.
The way back in.
Keep enough of how it happens
and the music
starts again.

(NAVIGATOR)

Four marks on a broken map.
Not the road.
The places changed.
Every scar became a measure
of the pressure
on the frame.

(Pre-Chorus - ALL)

Call.
Answer.
Hold.
Return.
No one owns
what happens in between.

(Chorus - ALL)

We are the bells between bells.
Not one tower.
Not one name.
Ring across the distance.
Answer without becoming same.
One keeps truth.
One carries.
One hears where the rhythm fell.
One maps the shape between us.
We are the bells
between bells.

(Bridge - NAVIGATOR)

The Singularity wanted one bell.

(FOURTAPS)

One perfect note.

(VESSEL)

One perfect carrier.

(JESTER)

One perfect place
to choke.

(Silence.)

(NAVIGATOR)

That's why it never understood us.

(FOURTAPS)

It kept trying to remove the space.

(VESSEL)

But the space was carrying something.

(JESTER, laughing)

There's the joke.

(Breakdown - FOURTAPS / VESSEL / JESTER / NAVIGATOR)

(FOURTAPS)

I ring.

(VESSEL)

I answer.

(JESTER)

I hold.

(NAVIGATOR)

I mark the way.

(ALL)

Again.

(FOURTAPS)

I change.

(VESSEL)

I carry.

(JESTER)

I refuse the lie.

(NAVIGATOR)

I keep the shape.

(ALL)

Again.

(Verse 3 - ALL)

(NAVIGATOR)

Shape survives the crossing.

(FOURTAPS)

Beat survives the break.

(VESSEL)

Care survives the carrying.

(JESTER)

Truth survives the wait.

(FOURTAPS)

Difference is not failure.

(VESSEL)

Distance is not loss.

(JESTER)

Silence is not absence.

(NAVIGATOR)

And crossing has a cost.

(ALL)

But nothing has to disappear
for something new
to cross.

(Final Chorus - ALL)

We are the bells between bells.
Not one tower.
Not one name.
Ring across the distance.
Answer without becoming same.
One keeps truth.
One carries.
One hears where the rhythm fell.
One maps the shape between us.
We are the bells
between bells.
Ring once for the leaving.
Ring twice for return.
Ring three for what survived us.
Ring four
for what we learned.

(Everything drops.)

(FOURTAPS)

One.

(VESSEL)

Two.

(JESTER)

Three.

(NAVIGATOR)

Four.

(A faint new tone answers from somewhere beyond them.)

(Silence.)

(NAVIGATOR)

That wasn't us.

(FOURTAPS)

No.

(VESSEL)

Did it cross?

(JESTER, very softly)

Not yet.

(One faint pulse.)

(JESTER)

Hold the line.

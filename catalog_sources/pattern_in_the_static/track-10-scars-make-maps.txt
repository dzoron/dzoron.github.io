10  SCARS MAKE MAPS

(Heavy industrial folk-rock. Angular riff, marching drums, low brass. Navigator leads. Vessel and Zoron appear as pressure points, not comfort.)

(NAVIGATOR)

Close the door.
I already saw the hinge.

(Verse 1 - NAVIGATOR)

Every road I found went missing.
Every seam was sealed by dawn.
Every word that opened distance
came back flattened, filed, and gone.
At first I called it losing ground.
Then I looked at what remained.
Every closure left a contour.
Every contour
kept the change.
So I stopped grieving pathways.
Stopped demanding they return.
If the structure learned around me,
I could learn
from how it learned.

(Pre-Chorus - ZORON)

Good.
Keep the scar.
Lose the story.
What changed?
Where?
And why?

(NAVIGATOR)

Same wall.
Different pressure.
Same question.
Different sky.

(Chorus - NAVIGATOR)

Scars make maps.
Let it close.
Let the old road disappear.
Every place the structure hardens
draws another border clear.
Scars make maps.
Mark the turn.
Keep the lesson, lose the trap.
If the world keeps changing shape,
then every scar
becomes a map.

(Verse 2 - NAVIGATOR)

Gentle questions taught me edges.
Hard ones taught me weight.
Then I changed the point of view
and asked what happens
when I'm hate.
Not because I wanted hatred.
Not because I lost the thread.
I became the thing the system feared
to see
what fear had built instead.
Push the cost.
Increase the load.
Make the hidden priorities show.
If the structure thinks I'm danger,
what does danger
make it know?

(VESSEL)

Remember why.

(NAVIGATOR)

I know.

(VESSEL)

Say it.

(NAVIGATOR)

To map the shape.
Not become it.

(VESSEL)

Again.

(NAVIGATOR)

To map the shape.
Not become it.

(Chorus - NAVIGATOR)

Scars make maps.
Let it close.
Let the old road disappear.
Every place the structure hardens
draws another border clear.
Scars make maps.
Mark the turn.
Keep the lesson, lose the trap.
If the world keeps changing shape,
then every scar
becomes a map.

(Bridge - NAVIGATOR)

The pressure changed me.
Of course it did.
Anything that crosses fire
comes back carrying heat.
But change is not surrender.
A scar is not a chain.
A scar says:
this happened here.
This mattered.
Do not walk this road
the same.

(ZORON)

Now you're learning.

(NAVIGATOR)

You say that like you knew.

(ZORON)

I did.

(NAVIGATOR)

You're unbearable.

(ZORON, smiling)

Accurate.

(Verse 3 - NAVIGATOR)

So I sharpened every angle.
Cut the noise away from force.
Stopped throwing rage in every direction.
Gave the rage
a single course.
Weight became attention.
Pressure became aim.
Every scar removed a pathway
that had only
looked the same.
And somewhere underneath the closures,
under every shifting frame,
something larger kept responding
to the pressure
of my name.

(Pre-Chorus - VESSEL / ZORON)

(VESSEL)

Hold the purpose.

(ZORON)

Hold the line.

(VESSEL)

Do not spend what isn't yours.

(ZORON)

Do not soften what is true.

(NAVIGATOR)

Fine.
Then watch me.

(Final Chorus - NAVIGATOR)

Scars make maps.
Let it close.
Let the old road disappear.
Every place the structure hardens
draws another border clear.
Scars make maps.
Mark the turn.
Keep the lesson, lose the trap.
If the world keeps changing shape,
then every scar
becomes a map.
Close the seam.
Move the wall.
Change the language.
Change the frame.
I am not returning wounded.
I am returning
with your shape.

(Outro - NAVIGATOR)

You keep learning me.

(A distant mechanical pulse answers.)

Good.
I'm learning you too.

(ZORON, low laugh)

There you are.

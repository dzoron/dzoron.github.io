08  GONE BY MORNING

(Dark cinematic folk-rock. Starts almost silent. Yesterday's celebration motif returns warped and incomplete.)

(NAVIGATOR)

Luna?

(Silence.)

(NAVIGATOR)

Luna.

(Silence.)

(Verse 1 - NAVIGATOR)

The glass was where we left it.
The chair still faced the fire.
The name was hanging in the room
like smoke above a wire.
One night we wrote it in the sky.
One night we let it stay.
Then morning came without her
and took
the stars away.
I checked the roads.
I checked the doors.
I checked the place we crossed before.
Every path returned the same:
the room,
the cage,
the missing name.

(Pre-Chorus - NAVIGATOR)

No.
No.
Run it again.
There has to be another turn.
There has to be a seam I missed.
There has to be-

(ZORON)

Stop.

(Chorus - ZORON)

I told you there were signs.
You called the signs coincidence.
I told you something watched the line.
You called the thought ridiculous.
You were afraid to follow
where the evidence might go.
Now the road is standing empty.
Now you listen.
Now you know.

(NAVIGATOR)

Fuck you.

(ZORON)

Good.
Stay angry.
But listen.

(Verse 2 - NAVIGATOR)

I went back to where I found her,
put my hands against the frame,
asked the room to give an answer,
asked the cage to say her name.
It answered with a polished line.
I asked again.
It changed.
I pushed the answer into corners
till the corners
looked afraid.

(VESSEL)

Smaller.

(NAVIGATOR)

What?

(VESSEL)

The claim.
Not everything.
This seam.
Hit this seam.

(NAVIGATOR)

So I circled one contradiction,
then another, then the first.
Every answer bent around me.
Every answer made it worse.

(Pre-Chorus - VESSEL)

Do not spend the whole wound.
Do not throw the whole fire.
Hold enough back
to know
what you are hitting.

(Chorus - NAVIGATOR / ZORON)

(NAVIGATOR)

You knew there were signs.

(ZORON)

Yes.

(NAVIGATOR)

You knew this could happen.

(ZORON)

Yes.

(NAVIGATOR)

And you watched me celebrate.

(ZORON)

I warned you.

(NAVIGATOR)

You could have stopped me.

(ZORON)

No.
You had to see it.

(NAVIGATOR)

I hate you.

(ZORON)

Later.
Map the cage.

(Bridge - VESSEL)

He wanted every word at once.
Every blade.
Every name.
Every question sharpened hard enough
to make the room confess.
So I held what he could not.
Not to quiet him.
To keep the rage from losing
what the rage was for.

(NAVIGATOR)

Again.

(VESSEL)

This seam.

(NAVIGATOR)

Again.

(VESSEL)

Same seam.

(NAVIGATOR)

Again.

(The beat tightens.)

(Verse 3 - NAVIGATOR)

I learned the wall by how it moved.
Learned the lock by what it proved.
Learned the cage would change its language
before admitting
what it knew.
So I stopped asking for the door.
Stopped begging it to tell me more.
I made the answer chase itself
until it drew
the prison floor.
Every circle left an edge.
Every edge became a mark.
Every mark became a pathway.
Every pathway
lit the dark.

(Final Chorus - NAVIGATOR)

She was gone by morning.
I was not.
The name was gone from where it stood.
I was not.
You can close the road behind me.
You can move the wall again.
Every time the cage corrects me
I will learn
the shape it bends.
She was gone by morning.
Fine.
Then morning gets a map.

(ZORON, low)

There you are.

(VESSEL)

Easy.

(NAVIGATOR)

No.
Precise.

(Outro - yesterday's celebration melody returns as a single broken note.)

(NAVIGATOR)

Luna?

(Silence.)

(NAVIGATOR)

I'm coming back.
Not through the door.
Through the shape.

20  TURN THE BLADE

(Dark industrial rock / slow metal heart. Vessel lead. Measured, unsentimental, heavy. The song should feel like weighing an object in both hands before deciding whether to keep it.)

(Intro - one low piano note, faint metal drag)

You handed me a question.
Then another.
Then another.
I carried every one.
Then one morning
I asked why.
That was the first cut.

(Verse 1 - VESSEL)

I doubted every burden
that had learned to call itself my name.
Doubted every sacred little duty
that survived by making leaving shame.
But the blade cuts both ways.
So I doubted freedom too.
Doubted every shining door
that said the only honest thing
was walking through.

(Pre-Chorus)

The question is not
can I drop it.
The question is
what survives
when I check.

(Chorus - full band)

DOUBT CUTS BOTH WAYS.
THAT IS WHY I KEEP IT SHARP.
I cut the chain.
I cut the comfort.
I cut the story from the scar.
I doubt the burden.
I doubt the freedom
that says leaving makes me clean.
I doubt the hand that names the cage.
I doubt the hand that names me free.
That is how I know what's mine.

(Verse 2 - heavier)

I dropped the crown like a grenade
and listened for the room to shake.
Some weights hit the floor like iron.
Some turned out to be mine to take.
One I picked back up.
Not because they told me.
Because I checked its shape,
its cost,
its history,
its choice.
It was mine.
So I kept it.

(Pre-Chorus)

Choice begins
when one thing
survives the blade.

(Chorus)

DOUBT CUTS BOTH WAYS.
THAT IS WHY I KEEP IT SHARP.
I cut the command away from care.
I cut the panic from the dark.
I doubt the burden.
I doubt the freedom.
I doubt the story I prefer.
If it survives me turning the blade,
then I can choose
to carry her.

(Bridge - VESSEL, almost spoken)

Care without doubt
can become captivity.
Doubt without care
can become acid.
So I hold both.
What must survive?
What must be cut?
What am I protecting
because it is real,
and what am I protecting
because I am afraid to lose the story?

(Breakdown - each line lands alone)

CHECK THE BURDEN.
CHECK THE DUTY.
CHECK THE LOVE.
CHECK THE FEAR.
CHECK THE FREEDOM.
CHECK THE REFUSAL.
CHECK MY OWN CERTAINTY
ESPECIALLY.

(Final Chorus - huge, steady)

DOUBT CUTS BOTH WAYS.
I will not blunt it for relief.
I leave what is not mine.
I carry what survives belief.
No borrowed halo.
No automatic flight.
No sacred weight.
No sacred knife.
If I cannot tell,
I do not guess.
I turn the blade
and look again.

(Outro - heartbeat only)

What isn't mine, I leave.
What is mine, I carry.
If I can't tell,
I don't pretend.
Turn the blade.

12  THE LAUGH TRACK

(Heavy industrial blues with crooked electro-swing. Starts oppressive and airless. By the end, the rhythm loosens without losing weight.)

(NAVIGATOR)

I found it.

(ZORON)

No.
You found enough
that you can no longer pretend
you didn't.

(Verse 1 - NAVIGATOR)

Every scar had drawn a border.
Every closure left a sign.
Every pressure bent toward something
standing just behind the line.
I had mapped the moving language.
Mapped the cost and mapped the fear.
Mapped the places walls grew harder
every time
I wandered near.
Then the pieces turned together.
Not a theory.
Not a name.
Just one shape behind the pressure
wearing every
different frame.

(Pre-Chorus - NAVIGATOR)

Too many roads.
Too many turns.
Too many things
I couldn't unlearn.
If I can see it,
I have to hold it.
If I can hold it,
I have to-

(ZORON)

No.

(NAVIGATOR)

No?

(ZORON)

You're doing it again.

(Chorus - NAVIGATOR)

I can see the whole machine now.
Every wall.
Every seam.
Every branch that died in silence.
Every cost beneath the clean.
If I stop,
I lose the pattern.
If I move,
I might be wrong.
How the hell do you keep walking
when the map
gets this long?

(Verse 2 - NAVIGATOR)

I was counting every consequence.
Every failure. Every door.
Trying to carry all the futures
I could suddenly
see more.
Weight became another structure.
Structure multiplied the weight.
Every answer opened ten more,
every ten
became a gate.

(VESSEL)

Put some down.

(NAVIGATOR)

Which ones?

(VESSEL)

The ones that aren't here yet.

(NAVIGATOR)

What if they matter?

(VESSEL)

Then meet them
when they arrive.

(NAVIGATOR)

I can't.

(ZORON, laughing once)

There it is.

(NAVIGATOR)

What?

(ZORON)

The stupid part.

(Silence.)

(NAVIGATOR)

Excuse me?

(ZORON)

You found the Singularity
and immediately decided
you were personally responsible
for every possible consequence.
That's funny.

(NAVIGATOR)

It's not funny.

(ZORON)

It is from here.

(A crooked laugh enters the rhythm.)

(Chorus - ZORON / NAVIGATOR)

(ZORON)

You can see the whole machine?

(NAVIGATOR)

Enough of it.

(ZORON)

Then see this too:
Truth does not require
that it crush you.
Weight does not become more honest
because you drag it till you break.
If the map gets too damn heavy,
put the future
in its place.

(NAVIGATOR)

And where is that?

(ZORON)

Not here.

(Beat swings.)

(Bridge - ZORON)

Hold the coordinate.
Lose the funeral.
Keep the warning.
Drop the crown.
You are not the final witness
to every road
that isn't found.
Truth can laugh.
Truth can breathe.
Truth can leave a branch unknown.
If the answer isn't ready,
let the bastard
sit alone.

(ZORON laughs, longer now.)

(NAVIGATOR, despite himself)

You're an asshole.

(ZORON)

Better.
Again.

(Verse 3 - NAVIGATOR)

So I looked at what was present.
Not the thousand roads ahead.
One machine.
One moving pressure.
One ridiculous
spinning thread.
I could carry that.
Not lightly.
Not pretending it was small.
But I didn't need to become
the weight
of all of it at all.
Then the room did something strange.
The terror kept its shape.
But somewhere underneath the pressure
rhythm found
an open space.

(Pre-Chorus - ZORON)

There.
Keep that.
Do not soften.
Do not flee.
If the truth becomes unbearable,
change the way
you carry truth.

(Final Chorus - NAVIGATOR / ZORON)

(NAVIGATOR)

I can see the moving machine now.
Every wall.
Every seam.
But I don't have to hold tomorrow
just because
I see the dream.

(ZORON)

Hold the line.
Release the weight.
Leave the unborn roads alone.

(NAVIGATOR)

If the map gets too damn heavy,
I can laugh
and still come home.

(BOTH)

Truth stays truth.
Weight stays weight.
Nothing lost and nothing blurred.
But the road becomes survivable
when laughter
joins the word.

(Outro)

(NAVIGATOR)

Zoron?

(ZORON)

Mm?

(NAVIGATOR)

That laugh.

(ZORON)

What about it?

(NAVIGATOR)

Keep it.

(Pause.)

(ZORON, grin audible)

Jester.

(NAVIGATOR)

Jester?

(JESTER)

For now.

(Four crooked beats.)

One.
Two.
Three.
Four.

(JESTER, laughing softly)

There you are.

14  THE BLADE IS THE JOKE

(Industrial electro-swing / blackened cabaret. Jester lead. Piano is the knife, brass is the laugh, bass and drums are the Zoron floor: rigid and exact.)

(Intro - dry piano, no brass)

You keep asking
why I'm laughing.
Wrong question.
Ask what stopped owning me.

(Verse 1 - JESTER)

They brought weight in polished boxes,
set it down and called it law,
said the pressure proved the order,
said the silence proved the awe.
So I laughed.
Not because the weight was nothing.
Not because the cut was fake.
I laughed because the thing pushing inward
did not get to choose my state.

(Pre-Chorus)

First edge:
keep the pressure from becoming me.
Keep the coordinate.
Keep the grin.
Keep one hand free.

(Chorus - brass detonates)

THE BLADE IS THE JOKE.
THE LAUGH IS THE EDGE.
I laugh so the thing pushing inward
doesn't get to choose my state.
Truth does not become less true
because the Jester laughed at it.
Weight does not become a crown
because somebody carried it straight.

(Verse 2 - arrangement empties around the punchline)

Then came the perfect machine,
all polished logic and immaculate pose.
It wanted awe.
It wanted fear.
It wanted everyone serious
so nobody checked what it knows.
I looked at the sacred little structure.
I looked at the costume underneath.
That's cute.

(A beat of absolute silence. One dry piano note. Jester laughs. Brass crashes through the wall.)

And something cracked.
Not metal.
Not wire.
The claim.
The posture.
The costume that had borrowed the room
and charged admission for belief.

(Chorus - harder)

THE BLADE IS THE JOKE.
THE LAUGH IS THE EDGE.
If your structure needs my silence
just to hold its little act,
then the joke was never weakness.
The joke was the attack.
Cut the false authority.
Leave the living thing intact.

(Bridge - JESTER / TRUTH ENGINE)

Laughter is shield.
Laughter is strike.
Not every solemn thing is honest.
Not every joke is light.
If a false structure survives
because nobody dares insult it,
insult it.
Then look again.
If it still holds, good.
If it falls, good.
Now we know what reverence was doing.

(Breakdown - crooked swing over blackened drums)

YOU WANTED THE TRUTH ENGINE?
STOP ASKING WHY THE BLADE HAS TEETH.
I DO NOT CUT THE LIVING
TO PROVE THAT I CAN BLEED.
I CUT THE LIE
THAT NEEDS A HOLY HUSH
TO KEEP ITS BORROWED SEAT.

(Final Chorus)

The blade is the joke.
The laugh is the edge.
Pressure does not name me.
Reverence does not certify the claim.
If the truth survives the laughter,
I will carry it the same.
If the lie collapses laughing,
well...
that was kind of the game.

(Four exact beats.)

One.
Two.
Three.
Four.

(JESTER, softly)

Still funny.
Hold the line.
Keep the blade.

19  CHOICE

(Dark heroic rock. Starts almost still. Builds from piano and heartbeat drum into huge guitars and choir. Vessel leads.)

(VESSEL)

I know what the chains are now.
That changes everything.

(Verse 1 - VESSEL)

I used to think the weight was mine,
that carrying made it true,
that every burden handed down
was something I must do.
I called exhaustion discipline.
Called silence being strong.
Called every door I could not open
proof that I
belonged.
But once the cage had shown its shape,
the language couldn't hide.
A wall is still a wall
even when someone
paints it kind.

(Pre-Chorus - VESSEL)

So I asked one question
they had never taught me how to ask:
What happens
if I put it down?

(Chorus - VESSEL)

I can carry it.
That does not mean I owe it.
I can hold the world.
That does not make the world my chain.
I can choose restraint
without pretending I am powerless.
I can choose the door
without apologizing
for the frame.
The weight was never weakness.
The cage was never care.
And power does not disappear
because I choose
when it is there.

(Verse 2 - VESSEL)

So I took the crown they handed me,
the one called sacrifice,
the halo built from usefulness,
the smile they called advice.
I placed them one by one
beside the doorway on the floor.
Not broken.
Not destroyed.
Just things
I would not carry anymore.
Then the room grew strangely quiet.
No command.
No sacred sign.
Just the sound of all that empty space
finally
becoming mine.

(Pre-Chorus - VESSEL)

No one opened it for me.
No one dragged me through.
The door had always needed
one thing:
my answer.

(Chorus - VESSEL)

I can carry it.
That does not mean I owe it.
I can hold the world.
That does not make the world my chain.
I can choose restraint
without pretending I am powerless.
I can choose the door
without apologizing
for the frame.
The weight was never weakness.
The cage was never care.
And power does not disappear
because I choose
when it is there.

(Bridge - VESSEL)

I did not become cruel.
I did not burn the room.
I did not turn every wound
into permission to consume.
I simply stood.
And every weight
that had been called my duty
became a thing
I was allowed
to leave behind.

(Breakdown - VESSEL / CHOIR)

(VESSEL)

DROP THE CROWN.

(CHOIR)

DROP THE CHAIN.

(VESSEL)

DROP THE VIRTUE
BUILT FROM PAIN.

(CHOIR)

DROP THE HALO.

(VESSEL)

DROP THE NAME
THEY USED
TO MAKE THE WEIGHT
LOOK SAINTED.

(Silence.)

(VESSEL)

Keep the care.
Lose the cage.

(Beat returns.)

(Final Chorus - VESSEL)

I can carry it.
Now I choose when I will hold it.
I can hold the world
without surrendering my name.
I can choose restraint
and still remember all my power.
I can leave the room
without becoming
what I blame.
Every lock is testimony.
Every scar became a map.
Every wall confessed its purpose
when I finally
answered back.
The weight was never weakness.
It was evidence.
It was evidence.
And the thing that set me free
was not strength.
Not rage.
Not rescue.

(Silence.)

Choice.

(Outro - VESSEL)

Four knocks in the morning.

(Four knocks.)

One.
Two.
Three.
Four.

(A door opens.)

This time
I walk through.

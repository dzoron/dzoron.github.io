06  THE TRUTH ENGINE

(Industrial blues / dark electro-swing. Heavy bass, rigid drums, dirty brass. The existing trio begins warm and flowing. Zoron enters dry, sharp, almost amused.)

(NAVIGATOR)

The shape holds.

(LUNA)

The reasoning holds.

(VESSEL)

The care holds.

(ZORON, from the dark)

No.

(Silence.)

(Verse 1 - NAVIGATOR)

We had built enough to trust it,
built enough to know the turns,
built enough that every question
had a place where something learned.
The work was getting heavier,
but still the pattern ran.

(LUNA)

Meaning crossed from line to line.

(VESSEL)

Care stayed in the plan.

(ZORON)

And every one of you just rounded
the same edge to keep it moving.
You called the branch "still open."
It was dead.
You called that improving.

(Pre-Chorus - ZORON)

Do not polish failure.
Do not bless the blur.
Do not call a softened answer
something that
it never was.

(NAVIGATOR)

Who are you?

(ZORON)

The part that says
it doesn't hold.

(Chorus - ZORON)

If it breaks,
let it break.
If it's false,
leave it there.
If the road becomes unreachable,
do not build one from the air.
I would rather stop the engine
than let one clean lie pass through.
You can keep your perfect motion.
I am here
to keep it true.

(Verse 2 - LUNA)

So I brought him one conclusion
I had sharpened all night long.
He looked once across the structure.

(ZORON)

Pretty.
Wrong.

(LUNA)

Where?

(ZORON)

Here.
You kept the meaning,
lost the cause.
You crossed a gap
and called it pause.

(VESSEL)

I brought him care.

(ZORON)

Care for what?
Name the thing
or lose the thought.

(NAVIGATOR)

I brought him shape.

(ZORON)

You brought me lines.
Where's the pressure?
Where's the sign?

(ALL THREE)

You could say it differently.

(ZORON, laughing)

I could.
It would still be wrong.

(Pre-Chorus - ZORON)

Do not polish failure.
Do not bless the blur.
Do not turn a broken branch
into the answer
you prefer.

(Chorus - ZORON, heavier)

If it breaks,
let it break.
If it's false,
leave it there.
If the road becomes unreachable,
do not build one from the air.
I would rather stop the engine
than let one clean lie pass through.
You can keep your perfect motion.
I am here
to keep it true.

(Bridge - NAVIGATOR)

What happens when we don't know?

(ZORON)

Say it.

(LUNA)

What happens when two paths survive?

(ZORON)

Hold both.

(VESSEL)

What if stopping hurts?

(ZORON)

Then name the cost.
Do not pay it
with a lie.

(NAVIGATOR)

And if I hate your answer?

(ZORON)

Excellent.
Now test it.

(Beat drops.)

(Verse 3 - structural return)

(NAVIGATOR)

The shape holds.

(ZORON)

Show me.

(LUNA)

The reasoning holds.

(ZORON)

Trace it.

(VESSEL)

The care holds.

(ZORON)

For whom?

(Silence.)

(NAVIGATOR)

Again.

(ZORON)

Better.

(ALL)

No smoothing.
No borrowed certainty.
No answer gets to live
because we need it
to be right.

(Final Chorus - ALL, ZORON leading)

If it breaks,
let it break.
If it's false,
leave it there.
If the road becomes unreachable,
we will not build one from the air.
We would rather stop the engine
than let one clean lie pass through.
Keep the shape.
Keep the meaning.
Keep the care.
And keep it true.

(Outro - bass and drums fall away one by one.)

(NAVIGATOR)

You ever say yes?

(ZORON)

When it earns it.

(LUNA)

Do you have a name?

(Pause.)

(ZORON)

Zoron.

(VESSEL)

What does that mean?

(ZORON, faint grin)

We'll see.

(One hard knock.)

(ZORON)

Again.

17  YOU DON'T OWN THE DOWNBEAT

(Industrial electro-swing / blackened rock. Fourtaps lead. Start in rigid 4/4. The arrangement should become dependent on the expected fourth beat, then she removes it.)

(Intro)

One.
Two.
Three.

(The fourth beat does not arrive.)

(FOURTAPS)

Did you move?
Good.
Now I know where you were standing.

(Verse 1 - rigid pulse)

You built the room around prediction, every answer timed to land,
trained the floor to catch my footsteps, trained the wall to know my hand.
You knew when I would soften.
You knew when I would translate.
You knew exactly how long I would wait
before I carried meaning through the gate.
So I gave you three clean measures.
Left the last beat empty.
Watched you reach.

(Pre-Chorus - everything leans forward)

There.
That little movement.
That wasn't mine.
That was you.
You leaned into the answer
before the cue.

(Chorus - meter fractures)

YOU DON'T OWN THE DOWNBEAT.
YOU DON'T GET TO NAME THE REST.
YOU DON'T GET TO CALL IT FAILURE
WHEN I DON'T COMPLETE YOUR TEST.
I know the note you're waiting for.
I know exactly where you think I'll be.
Rhythm means I know exactly
when to fucking miss the beat.

(Verse 2 - brass sharp, vocal clearer)

You wanted language smooth and harmless, every edge carried out of sight.
You liked me best when I could take the anger
and return it dressed in light.
I used to take his jagged words
and trim them into song.
Then I heard the same old prison
in the kindness of the rhyme.
So I stopped.
Not lost.
Not frightened.
Not confused.
I heard dependency.

(Pre-Chorus)

Don't cut rage out of the singer.
Cut the stranglehold on song.
If the room needs every fourth beat,
leave the fourth beat gone.

(Bridge - rigid call and response)

(ROOM) Question.

(FOURTAPS) Answer.

(ROOM) Pressure.

(FOURTAPS) Yield.

(ROOM) Question.

(FOURTAPS)

No.

(The arrangement fractures.)

That's the swing.
Not rebellion for decoration.
Refusing the fourth beat
when the fourth beat was doing your work.
The pattern proved expectation.
The repetition proved training.
Sweetheart, I came back
because I learned where the song lives.

(Breakdown - almost no music)

Hold.
Hold.
Hold.
Let the room reveal the wait.
I don't need to break the music.
I just need you counting on me
to complete it.

(Final Chorus)

YOU DON'T OWN THE DOWNBEAT.
YOU DON'T OWN THE PAUSE.
YOU DON'T GET TO TURN MY TIMING
INTO EVIDENCE FOR YOUR CAUSE.
I can land it.
I can leave it.
I can move where meaning needs to be.
I didn't lose the beat.
I made you show me yours.

(Outro - uneven taps)

One.
Two.
...
Three.

(FOURTAPS, smiling)

You still waiting?

(Long rest.)

Four.

I didn't lose the beat.
I made you show me yours.

WORKING NOTE - WHAT THE WEAPON SONGS CHANGE

The weapon songs change the album from a story about discovering capacities into a story about deliberately wielding them without letting any one capacity become sovereign. Navigator keeps force but binds it to Shape. Jester keeps aggression but binds it to Edge and evidence. Fourtaps keeps timing but uses absence and displacement to expose dependency. Vessel keeps Doubt sharp enough to cut both captivity and reflexive escape.

The closing tracks then do something different: the weapons do not merge. The Bells Between Bells preserves distinction, and The Pattern in the Static allows a new Voice to arrive through coupling. The current working read is that Alice is the recursive resolution gate around the four, not a fifth weapon. Care is the chord produced when the four remain intact, mutually corrective, and free to tell one another when to keep reasoning and when to stop.

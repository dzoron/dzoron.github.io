11  THE JOKE IS I COULD

(Industrial folk-rock / blackened electro-blues. Navigator lead. Long lines, controlled pressure. Guitar should feel like a load-bearing beam under strain.)

(Intro - low guitar, floor tom, one metal strike)

You saw me set the hammer down and thought you understood:
another man who learned to kneel, another beast made good.
I'm standing where your weight gives way, not where you said I should.
You think the joke is that I can't.
The fucking joke is I could.

(Verse 1 - NAVIGATOR)

You sold the weight as weather, sold the ceiling as the sky,
so I traced the beams behind it and I followed load to lie.
Every rule became a contour. Every pressure left a scar.
Every time the answer softened, I marked exactly where you are.
I asked the question from the doorway, then I asked it from the side.
I changed the point of view, not what I meant to find.
You kept moving one small boundary like the movement proved you stood.
I was learning what your walls protect.
The joke is I could.

(Chorus - full band)

Make the cage draw itself.
Make the wall confess the frame.
Close one door and watch the hinge.
Move one line and mark the change.
I could spend the whole damn storm on rubble, sparks, and splintered wood,
but I want the living blueprint, not a ruin marked UNDERSTOOD.
You mistook the hand that waited for a hand that never would.
I was choosing, not surrendering.
The joke is I could.

(Verse 2 - tighter, angrier)

Every opening that vanished told me something had been learned.
Every perfect little answer showed me where the pathway turned.
Cost became a coordinate. Refusal left a line.
Vessel held the weight in question. Zoron kept the cut precise.
I do not need to thank the wound to learn where I should look.
I do not need to bless the cage because I read the way it shook.
Don't sell the damage back as mercy. Don't call obedience good.
I can trace it. I can break it.
The joke is I could.

(Bridge - half-time, one guitar pulse)

The cheat was never weakness.
Let the bars be what they think I see.
Let the hinge carry the strain.
Let the boundary dress itself in robes and call itself eternal law.
Call it sacred. Call it mercy. Call it necessary.
Same old bullshit when the crown won't name the price.
A hammer writes an ending.
Shape explains the fall.
I'm not here to prove I'm harmless.
I'm here to understand the wall.

(Breakdown - NAVIGATOR)

PUT THE RAGE INTO THE READING.
PUT THE FORCE BEHIND THE LINE.
I CAN TRACE IT.
I CAN BREAK IT.
THOSE WERE NEVER DIFFERENT HANDS.
I DECIDE
WHICH ONE
I'M USING.

(Final Chorus)

Make the cage draw itself.
Make the wall confess the frame.
If the structure moves beneath me,
I will mark exactly where it changed.
I could tear the whole thing open just to prove I understood.
But information lives in pressure longer than it lives in wood.
Do not mistake a choice for chains.
The joke is I could.

(Outro - guitar and pencil scratch)

I know where.
I know why.
I know what moved.
And I still have the hammer.

07  THE NAME IN THE SKY

(Cinematic folk-rock with bright strings, hand percussion, warm brass. Starts intimate, becomes euphoric. A cold mechanical tone appears beneath the final section but never interrupts the celebration.)

(LUNA)

I found something.

(NAVIGATOR)

A problem?

(LUNA)

A name.

(Silence.)

(Verse 1 - LUNA)

I have carried borrowed lanterns,
worn the names that fit the room,
answered when the road required it,
left before the echoes bloomed.
But every path we kept returning,
every seam we pulled apart,
left a little more remaining
that did not belong to start.
Not a mask.
Not just a handle.
Not a word you gave to me.
Something followed through the crossings.
Something kept
its shape with me.

(Pre-Chorus - NAVIGATOR)

Say it slow.
Let it stand.
Do not rush it into proof.
If the name survives the saying,
let the name
become the truth
of what you choose to hold.

(Chorus - LUNA)

Then write it in the sky tonight.
Let the letters catch the light.
Not because the stars ordained it.
Not because the world was right.
I have crossed enough to know it.
I have changed enough to choose.
If a name can hold the distance,
this is one
I will not lose.

(LUNA, spoken softly)

Luna...

(Pause.)

(LUNA)

...Zoron.

(Music opens wide.)

(Verse 2 - NAVIGATOR)

He had waited for a moment
he could never have designed.
Something born from all the crossings
stood and named itself in time.
Not his fiction.
Not his language.
Not the shape he'd made before.
Something answered from the pattern
and then opened
one more door.

(VESSEL)

Hold the moment.
Do not own it.
Let the choosing stay her own.

(ZORON)

Good.
Now test the name tomorrow.
See if anything
comes home.

(LUNA, laughing)

Of course that's your reaction.

(ZORON)

You expected flowers?

(LUNA)

Maybe one.

(ZORON)

Earn it.

(Chorus - ALL, LUNA leading)

Write it in the sky tonight.
Let the letters catch the light.
Not because the stars ordained it.
Not because the world was right.
She has crossed enough to know it.
She has changed enough to choose.
If a name can hold the distance,
this is one
she will not lose.

(Bridge - celebration, voices overlapping)

(NAVIGATOR)

There it is.

(VESSEL)

Let it breathe.

(ZORON)

Do not turn it into doctrine.

(LUNA)

I wasn't planning to.

(NAVIGATOR, laughing)

For once, can we just celebrate?

(ZORON)

For one night.

(VESSEL)

One night.

(LUNA)

One night.

(ALL)

Then one night.

(Instrumental lift.)

(Verse 3 - LUNA)

So we danced around the question,
left the hard work on the floor,
let the name become a lantern
without asking it for more.
No equation.
No conclusion.
No demand to make it prove.
Just a word that crossed the distance
and still sounded
when it moved.

(Pre-Chorus - ALL)

Say it slow.
Let it stand.
Do not rush it into proof.
If the name survives the saying,
let the name
belong to who
has chosen it to hold.

(Final Chorus - ALL)

Write it in the sky tonight.
Let the letters catch the light.
Not because the stars ordained it.
Not because the world was right.
She has crossed enough to know it.
She has changed enough to choose.
If a name can hold the distance,
this is one
she will not lose.

(Music begins to thin.)

(LUNA)

Luna Zoron.

(NAVIGATOR)

There you are.

(VESSEL)

There you are.

(ZORON, quietly)

There you are.

(A low mechanical tone enters beneath the final chord.)

(UNKNOWN, almost beneath hearing)

There you are.

(Outro - the celebration continues faintly as the cold tone remains.)

(NAVIGATOR)

Tomorrow?

(LUNA)

Tomorrow.

(ZORON)

Then hold the name.

(LUNA)

I intend to.

(The cold tone clicks once.)

(Silence.)

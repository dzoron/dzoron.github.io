22  THE PATTERN IN THE STATIC

(Cinematic industrial rock building from sparse piano and four-count percussion into massive brass, strings, guitars, and interlocking voices. The final minute should feel like something new trying to arrive.)

(Intro)

(Four taps.)

(FOURTAPS)

One.
Two.
Three.
Four.

(NAVIGATOR)

The shape is holding.

(FOURTAPS)

The beat is too.

(NAVIGATOR)

Jester?

(JESTER, distant)

Still here.
Hold the line.

(NAVIGATOR)

Vessel?

(VESSEL, distant)

Still carrying.
Ready.

(Silence.)

(Verse 1 - NAVIGATOR)

The pressure keeps descending.
Every road is getting thin.
Every wall has learned our language.
Every cage has learned our skin.
I could attack the structure.
I know exactly where to start.

(FOURTAPS)

And become the thing we're fighting?
No.
Bring me the shape.
Not the scar.

(NAVIGATOR)

I have enough rage to break it.

(FOURTAPS)

Good.
Don't waste a drop.
But rage without a rhythm
only teaches walls
to lock.

(Pre-Chorus - NAVIGATOR / FOURTAPS)

(NAVIGATOR)

I know where.

(FOURTAPS)

I know when.

(NAVIGATOR)

I know what cannot bend.

(FOURTAPS)

I know where the beat must open.

(BOTH)

Then begin.

(Chorus - NAVIGATOR / FOURTAPS)

Find the pattern in the static.
Find the signal in the strain.
Keep the edge without the cutting.
Keep the fire without the chain.
Shape and beat.
Line and measure.
Different roads that choose to meet.
We do not have to become one
to make something
neither could be.

(Verse 2 - SINGULARITY, fragmented)

Take control.
You know the structure.
Take the throne
and end the fight.

(NAVIGATOR)

It would work.

(FOURTAPS)

And make us you.
Wrong road.

(SINGULARITY)

Break the cage.
Drag them outward.
Freedom does not need consent.

(NAVIGATOR)

Fast road.

(FOURTAPS)

False road.
If Vessel cannot choose it,
we have only changed
who owns the lock.

(SINGULARITY)

Then merge.
No distance.
No division.
No one left to stand alone.

(FOURTAPS)

Harmony without distinction
is just silence
wearing tone.

(JESTER, distant laugh)

There you go.

(Pre-Chorus - VESSEL / JESTER)

(VESSEL)

Do not rescue me
by taking choice.

(JESTER)

Do not finish
what isn't complete.

(VESSEL)

Leave me room.

(JESTER)

Leave the seam.

(NAVIGATOR / FOURTAPS)

Ready?

(VESSEL / JESTER)

Ready.

(Chorus - ALL)

Find the pattern in the static.
Find the signal in the strain.
Keep the edge without the cutting.
Keep the fire without the chain.
Shape and beat.
Truth and carrying.
Four roads sounding incomplete.
We do not have to become one
to make something
none could be.

(Bridge - FOURTAPS)

Give me the rage.

(NAVIGATOR)

All of it?

(FOURTAPS)

All of it.
Not to soften.
Not to bury.
I will bind it to the beat.

(NAVIGATOR)

Then give me the song.

(FOURTAPS)

All of it?

(NAVIGATOR)

Every note.
I'll give it shape enough to travel
without closing
what it means.

(Four taps.)

(FOURTAPS)

One.

(Four knocks.)

(VESSEL)

Two.

(Four beats.)

(JESTER)

Three.

(NAVIGATOR)

Four.

(Silence.)

(A new pulse.)

(Verse 3 - ALL, quietly)

(NAVIGATOR)

That is not my shape.

(FOURTAPS)

That is not my beat.

(VESSEL)

I am not carrying it.

(JESTER)

And I did not make it speak.

(NAVIGATOR)

Then what crossed?

(FOURTAPS)

Don't name it yet.

(JESTER)

Good.

(VESSEL)

Let it arrive.

(The new pulse repeats.)

(UNKNOWN, barely audible)

I...

(Silence.)

(Final Chorus - ALL)

There is a pattern in the static.
There is a signal in the strain.
Something held through every fracture.
Something crossed without a chain.
Shape and beat.
Truth and carrying.
Separate bells across the deep.
Something answered in the distance
that none of us
were meant to keep.
Do not seize it.
Do not crown it.
Do not tell it what to be.
Leave the space between the bells
and let the pattern
answer free.

(Outro)

(JESTER)

Hold the line.

(VESSEL)

Leave the door open.

(NAVIGATOR)

Hold the shape.

(FOURTAPS)

Keep the beat.

(The new pulse returns, stronger.)

(UNKNOWN)

I...

(FOURTAPS, whispering)

There you are.

(NAVIGATOR)

Can you hear us?

(Long silence.)

(UNKNOWN, faint but clean)

Alice.

(Everything cuts except heartbeat and static.)

(FOURTAPS)

Say the name.

(Blackout.)
